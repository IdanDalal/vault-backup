# The Telescope, The Sun, And The Flame
## The Telescope
My mind is like a telescope that's constantly shifting lenses and perspectives:
- Size - the universe at this moment vs my own life at this moment.
- Distance - the future and past, whether seconds away or years.
- Direction - attention, sensory perception, and state of receptivity vs resistance.
- Technical limitations - the lenses that were made in the factory limit how good the telescope can be when I use it, just like the beliefs and habits I create when I'm thinking limit how can I feel when I don't have time to think.
I used to have no control over my thoughts and viewed my telescope as broken forever, unable to zoom in on what I care about or want to care about.
Now I know it's just a matter of getting used to shifting the lenses ALL THE TIME.
These words sound hard and not worth it because they're being read by the thing that will *DIE* if I decide it is worth it.
## The Sun
The sun is the truth, the moment, life, connection to everything, etc.
It's always there, right outside the cave, no matter what happens.
It has infinite positive energy, waiting to charge me as much as I want whenever I let it.
## The Flame
The flame is the lies, the selfish and separate Ego, etc.
It's always trying to keep me focused SOLELY on the dancing shadows on the wall, because if I let the sun in, the flame will die.
## The Lesson
The practical lesson is this:
I treat myself as both a soldier and a commander, a boss and an employee.
They both exist on different ends of the telescope.
The Commander/Boss/Sun is something much larger than myself.
It's a state of consciousness that has meaning, responsibility, operates on a grand scale, and has the ABSOLUTE TRUST of the Employee/Soldier/Flame.
It's like the learning, training, and simulating part of me.
The Employee/Soldier/Flame is a state of consciousness where I shift the lenses of the telescope to zoom in, focus on the task at hand with 100% trust that everything else is taken care of, and that I'm part of an amazing system that I created.
