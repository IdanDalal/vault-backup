The 16:00 autocommit fired and succeeded, and it committed `.vault-config/audit/edits-2026-09.jsonl`, whose two entries timestamp 12:32Z and 12:49Z, which is 15:32 and 15:49 local. Everything after 14:29 ran. Nothing before it did.

This is the diagnosis I offered for the 08-29 hole on 08-30 and could not confirm. It is now confirmed for 09-01 by a boot timestamp rather than by inference from resumption.

**Precision on what the boot record proves.** It proves the current boot started 14:29 on 09-01, so anything earlier ran under a different boot or none. It does not prove the machine was continuously down from 08-31 evening to 14:29. A prior boot could have been running with cron dead. The reason to prefer downtime: both jobs resumed on schedule under the new boot, `snapshot-2026-09-02` written at exactly 00:00 and this run at 09:00:01, which is what a power gap looks like and not what a broken scheduler looks like.

**The part that matters beyond the log.** Two scheduler gaps in five days, both from the box not being up. This is the first hard evidence the vault has about its own host's uptime, and it lands directly on an open decision.
