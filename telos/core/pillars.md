# 27 Prime Pillars

> Partner in the Pursuit of Personal Potential as Purpose

## A. Permanent Posterity
*Archive, Daily Notes, & BuJo*

### 01 Past & Pride
**Domain**: Archive

I record, document, and remember as much as I can.
I keep a detailed chronicle of my thoughts, decisions, and actions.
I recall where everything is, so I can access the info I keep here whenever I need it, like a second brain.
I let the papers get it wrong, because I **KNOW** the history books will get it right.

### 02 Process & Progress
**Domain**: Daily Notes

I track my daily journey with intention.
I capture fleeting thoughts before they dissolve.
I review my progress to maintain momentum.

### 03 Plans & Predictions
**Domain**: Bullet Journal

I plan with clarity and adapt with flexibility.
I set intentions and measure outcomes.
I learn from the gap between prediction and reality.

---

## B. Profound Purpose
*Identity, Meaning, & Growth*

### 04 Path & Philosophy
**Domain**: Ikigai, Meaning, & Spirituality

I walk through the world with ease.
I keep myself good company for both the journey and the destination.
I face *EVERY SINGLE ONE* of my Waterloos.

### 05 Profile & Preferences
**Domain**: Idi-entity, Inspiration, & Advice

I remember who I am.
I remember why I'm here.
I remember my core purpose, the best advice I ever got, and more.

### 06 Practice & Pedagogy
**Domain**: Learning, Skills, Knowledge, Mental Toolboxes

I learn new things every day.
I take full advantage of my unrestricted access to the entirety of human knowledge through the internet and AI.
I grow, evolve, change, and improve both my own life, and others'.

---

## C. Proactive Prudence
*Mindfulness, Control, & Resilience*

### 07 Prayer & Presence
**Domain**: Wu Wei, Meditation, & Mindfulness

I meditate every single day.
I remain mindful of my body.
I remain conscious of my actions.

### 08 Promises & Prevention
**Domain**: Control, Quitting, & Habits

I am free.
I am in control.
I make my own decisions.
I choose my own habits.
I stay conscious, sober, and *SHARP*.

### 09 Problems & Pain
**Domain**: Kintsugi, Limitations, & Failures

I accept my limitations and failures.
I do **NOT** embrace them or indulge in them.
I learn from them, and move on.
I do not identify with my current and temporary situation.
I embody the concept of Kintsugi.
I repair with gold, improve, and add value to broken things.

---

## D. Primal Prerequisites
*Survival Foundations*

### 10 Palate & Provisions
**Domain**: Food, Cooking

I give my body the ingredients for a fit and healthy lifestyle by learning about food, expanding my menu, and enjoying cooking.
I make an effort to balance mostly healthy food with a minimal, but still existent amount of delicious food.

### 11 Pause & Patience
**Domain**: Rest, Sleep

I pace myself according to my body's needs and limits.
I stay effective, energetic, & dynamic.

### 12 Planet & Patrol
**Domain**: Nature, Travel, & Off-Screen Reality

I "Get Rendered" outside my concrete habitat.
I spend time outside in beautiful nature where life happens.
I feel the sun, breathe fresh air, listen to the Earth.
I travel the world and explore geographical opportunities.

---

## E. Physical Power
*Body & Health*

### 13 Peak & Perspiration
**Domain**: Fitness, Sports, & Movement

I exert energy every day to keep my body in peak physical condition and to maximize my readiness for demanding experiences and situations.
I make an effort to find activities I enjoy, like VR, hip-hop dancing, walking, and more.

### 14 Presentation & Perfection
**Domain**: Hygiene

I maintain my appearance with intention.
Hair, beard, skin, teeth - all reflect self-respect.

### 15 Purity & Prescriptions
**Domain**: Doctors, Medical

I attend to my health proactively, not reactively.
I keep medical appointments and follow through on care.

---

## F. Prized Possessions
*Environment & Resources*

### 16 Palace & Periphery
**Domain**: Apartment

I make sure everything is in its place, and there's a place for every thing.
I keep my apartment clean and organized.

### 17 Purchases & Premiums
**Domain**: Finances, Subscriptions

I manage my finances responsibly.
I maximize value from my purchases and subscriptions.

### 18 Plunder & Property
**Domain**: Physical Toolboxes

I use the right tools for the right tasks at the right time.

---

## G. Passionate Pursuits
*Recreation, Entertainment, & Fun*

### 19 Play & Pleasure
**Domain**: Hobbies

I pursue activities that engage me, contribute to my life, help me be present through action, and enrich my taste as a creator.
I follow my internal fun compass, but guide it through curated lists and conditions to maximize each experience and absorb it fully.

### 20 Prose & Pioneering
**Domain**: Write, Generate, Create

I express my creative freedom by writing Mini-Essays, Reviews, and more.
I make sure the experience is poignant and memorable for the reader by using AI tools to improve my writing.

### 21 Publish & Post
**Domain**: Sharing

I generate images, videos, and voiceover to present my writing in video format.
I share my creations with the world.

---

## H. Prominent People
*Interactions, Connections, & Relationships*

### 22 Party & Pack
**Domain**: Family, Friends

I create and maintain positive and impactful relationships with people I love.

### 23 Partner & Pair
**Domain**: Spouse, Romance, Marriage

I am a worthy partner to my future wife.
I become a better man every day.
I plan and prepare for a shared life with a woman I love.

### 24 Participate & Perform
**Domain**: Strangers, Socializing

I constantly seek interactions that improve my social skills, enrich and enhance my experiences, and make my connections feel natural and fulfilling.
I express myself deeply and genuinely in my interactions, instead of offering others a shallow reflection of themselves.
I throw shine instead of shade by giving authentic and enthusiastic compliments when I feel them.
I'm always the first one to smile.

---

## I. Paradoxical Philanthropy
*Mission, Giving, & Helping*

### 25 Profession & Payment
**Domain**: Vocation, Contribution, & Income

I engage in modern capitalist society by working and being financially compensated.
I create value that others willingly exchange for.

### 26 Projects & Prospects
**Domain**: Side-hustles

I pursue projects that align with my skills and interests.
I explore opportunities for growth and income beyond primary work.

### 27 Price & Penitence
**Domain**: Selfless Sacrifices

I create and share things that give value to strangers.
I help others solve problems and achieve goals.
I give without expectation of return.
