Watch best-in-class carefully constructed narrative artworks (Stranger Things - 42 episodes over a decade, one good story, 10 >9.0-rated episodes) AND unfocused made-by-committee products (Fear The Walking Dead - 113 episodes in 8 years, many bad stories, 17 <6-rated episodes).

### Critique
All of them. Some in writing, some in thoughts.

### Create
Ideas that aim to replicate the highest highs of masterpieces' emotional climaxes while providing intellectual returns on intellectual investments through sophisticated, surprising stories.
