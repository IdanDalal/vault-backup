# Beneficiaries

*All work serves people. These are the people. Everything else is means.*

---

## Core Principle

> **I am selective with time, energy, attention, and resources because they are finite.**
> Every project must trace back to a beneficiary, or it is vanity—not mission.

---

## Checkpoint Protocol

Before starting any project, answer:

| Question | Acceptable Answer | If No Answer |
|----------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Who does this serve?** | Name from hierarchy | Project is vanity |
| **What do they get?** | Concrete deliverable | Project is abstract |
| **When do they get it?** | Date or milestone | Project is indefinite |

**If any answer is missing → do not start.**

---

The work is not for:
- ~~Me right now~~
- ~~My future self~~
- ~~Abstract "everyone"~~

The work **is** for:
- Specific people I love (family)
- Specific people I will find (students)
- Specific people I will create (children)

**Technical excellence is the means. Human flourishing is the end.**
