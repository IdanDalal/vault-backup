## Custom Terminology

### Get Rendered™
Going outside, interacting with living physical creatures. Life is a video game where staying alone means the world renders without you. Getting rendered = participating in reality.

### Cash Out™
Executing after preparation. Example: Practiced hip-hop lyrics at home (cashing in), then performed at clubs when DJ played those songs (cashing out) - muscle memory handles lyrics, attention freed for expression.

### Waterloos
Actions avoided for so long that doing them means facing certain "defeat." Not single events but committing to entire journeys that include failure. Named after Napoleon's final defeat.

### The Champion Moment™
Peak effort with no audience. Sweat, strain, faint smile - doing the hard thing when no one is watching.
