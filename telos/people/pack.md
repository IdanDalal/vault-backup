## What Happened to Past Friendships (Reframed 2026-01-14)

### The Mask Pattern → Market Exit

**Original frame:** "I stopped wearing the mask and got punished."

**Functional frame:** Ran an experiment N times with consistent results:
- Suppress authentic self → acceptance
- Express authentic self → rejection

**Conclusion:** Those specific people couldn't handle authentic self. That's a **compatibility verdict**, not a **self verdict**.

### The Exchange Rate

From interview:
> "I stopped communicating because the price was too high. I want to live an expressive life as long as the price is low."

| Market Condition | Decision |
|------------------|----------|
| Inauthenticity required | Price too high → exit |
| Authenticity punished | Price too high → exit |
| Authenticity accepted | Price acceptable → engage |

**Current state:** Waiting for markets with better exchange rates. Not defeat — strategic withdrawal.
