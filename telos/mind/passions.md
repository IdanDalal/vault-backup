## Quality Criteria

**The Completionist Consumption Pattern:**

Never skip fillers. Never fast-forward bad parts. This generates comprehensive pattern recognition:

| Knowledge Layer | Examples |
|-----------------|----------|
| **Rules of the game** | Cinematography, kinesthetics, thematic cohesion |
| **Good moves / bad moves** | Tropes, principles, narrative patterns |
| **Good players / bad players** | Insomniac vs Ubisoft, Villeneuve vs Bay |

Why consume both masterpieces AND lowest-common-denominator products?
→ You learn rules by watching them followed AND broken.
