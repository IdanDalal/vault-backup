# Ten more rooms to get rendered in (social activities research, 2026-10-22)

Idi's question: 37, Petah Tikva, only regular social outlet = dancing alone on the hip-hop floor of one club, Thu/Fri, in free at 22:45-22:55. What are 10 more activities to start?

**Verdict: keep the club for joy and synchrony, and add rooms where the same people return every week, talk is possible, and a task or a role carries the first words.** Ten picks below, ordered as a ladder from low to high social demand, slotted around Thu/Fri nights, ₪0 except one.

Method: 5 agents. 2 read all 42 `telos/` files and the rest of the vault for a profile; 3 researched online (friendship science, ~45 sources; Gush Dan listings, ~70 options; free-entry and paid-role formats, 34 items). jep re-checked the three nearest dates by hand on 2026-10-22: Hakvutza intro (confirmed), ACX meetup (confirmed), TAMI open night (no 2026 date found, call first). Raw research: `projects/social-activities-2026-10-22/`.

## 1. Selection rules

Evidence rules (from the friendship, autism and anxiety research):

| # | Rule | Confidence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Recurring beats one-off: same people, same slot, weekly | high | Back 2008 (random seating, friendship 1 yr later); Moreland & Beach 1992 (attendance alone raised liking); Pearce 2015 |
| R2 | Shared task first, talk second | medium-high | Wright 1982 (side-by-side friendship); Koegel 2013; Taylor & Alden 2011 (safety behaviours = things anxious people do to feel safe, which make partners like them less) |
| R3 | Talk must be physically possible | medium | Hall 2019 (talk/joke/play hours predict friendship); Dunbar 2017 (a "local" with known regulars = more close friends) |
| R4 | Synchrony bonds you to the people you moved with, so a class or crew beats solo in a crowd | medium (lab effect real, size likely inflated) | Tarr 2015, 2016; Rennung & Göritz 2016; Atwood 2022 |
| R5 | Interest-matched rooms let content beat first-impression style bias | medium | Sasson 2017 (bias vanished on transcripts); Koegel 2013; Atherton 2024 (D&D, autistic adults, n~8) |
| R6 | A helping role is the low-threat on-ramp; 2+ h/week is the dose that tracked lower loneliness | medium-low (1 RCT; volunteering panels are 50+, observational) | Trew & Alden 2015; Carr 2018; HRS 2023 |
| R7 | Climb a graded ladder, easiest rung first | high on principle | Mayo-Wilson 2014 (CBT, 101 trials) |
| R8 | Include one autistic-majority room; disclosure raised rapport | medium-low (2025 replication weakened the 2020 headline) | Crompton 2020, 2025; Watts 2024 |

## 7. What this list does not fix

1. Contact alone was the weakest lever in loneliness trials; changing expectations of rejection did more (Masi 2011, mostly older adults; Hickin 2021, 28 RCTs, g ~0.43 secondhand). Cheap version: after each outing, one line "feared / happened".
2. "Crippling social anxiety" (`telos/identity/essence.md`): individual CBT has stronger evidence than any activity here (Mayo-Wilson 2014). Whether his health fund covers it: unverified.
3. Hours: ~40-60 shared leisure hours per person before casual friendship (Hall 2019; self-report, unreplicated, low confidence on the numbers). A weekly 2-3 h room reaches it in ~4-6 months; a monthly room never does alone.
4. Distance: 7 of the 10 sit in Tel Aviv / Ramat Gan, 15-30 min by scooter. Petah Tikva itself offered little (Makefet adult calendar = lectures; Beat To Dance adult slot women-only).
5. Unverified: TAMI 2026 schedule, One Mic 2026 status, shelter hours, Fluent next date, Game Dev TLV price, Hakvutza course fee, the benefit-line figure.
6. Excluded on his filters: salsa/bachata socials (₪50-60), speed dating (₪125-135, 1:1), run clubs (08:00 Friday; dance already covers movement), Tel Aviv municipal running groups (residents only), board-game bars and LVL UP barcade (alcohol-centred, paid), PEERS course (instructor-led, parent component, paid).
