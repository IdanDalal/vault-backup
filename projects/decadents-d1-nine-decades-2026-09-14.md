## Findings

- Sealed heads: 61 of 72. The tail "no face, no skin visible anywhere, gloved hands" holds for object heads; masks and gas masks leak eyes.
- Film stock per decade landed on the first try in every decade (silent-film halation, FSA grain, Speed Graphic flash, Kodachrome, Ektachrome, Polaroid frame, Kodak Gold flash, 3 MP JPEG, VSCO warmth). Medium confidence that these read as the decade to a viewer; they read so to jep.
- "Pixelated cube" drifts to a CRT (57, 58) or to Minecraft (63, 64); a skull or censor-block texture stays on-brief.
- Body skin appears where the costume exposes it (sequins, shorts, tie-dye); say "long sleeves" or "covered" when skin matters.
