## Room state → move

| | Tell | Move |
|---|---|---|
| **Flat** | You're talking most. Polite, correct, unsurprising. | Add a block. **Not** more energy. |
| **Repetitive** | They've got the shape and are performing it. | Change a rule. Reverse who asks. |
| **Chaotic** | Loud, jumping, nothing lands twice. | **Count to five first** — dissipating or exapting? Then remove a block + add a constraint. |
| **Organized** | They build on each other, not on you. | Stop talking. |

**Before all four: is she oriented?** Where am I · what's this got to do with me · what may I do next.
Session order: **orientation → connection → navigation → narration.** *Tell me what happened* comes last.

## Rules

- Join the unit. Never convene.
- Name in front of the verb. «D — jump.»
- One column on the board. Never two numbers.
- Nobody compared to anybody. Praise specific, never relative.
- Copying → change the geometry, never issue a rule.
- Pointing and drawing always count.
- Be wrong out loud and enjoy it.
- **Every word points at something in the room.** *You have five fingers*, never *Johnny has five apples*.
- Back off a rung faster than 1-on-1. Never end on a miss.
- **Every guess gets answered before you leave.** Open tasks go home; open questions don't.
- **Whatever you sound like when she's wrong is what she'll sound like to herself.** Not the words — the sound.
- Leave while it's still working.
- Mom passes through → invite, never assign. «בא לך לשמוע כמה מילים באנגלית הן כבר יודעות? תראי את הלוח.»

## Stations

**The hunt** — default when flat
«אם לא הייתן פה עכשיו — איפה הייתן, ומה הייתן עושות?» · «ראיתי אתכן פעם רוקדות פה — מה השיר?»
Detonate once, jointly: *between the two of you — twenty-six English words, in this room, right now.*

**The room is already English**
«יש פה יותר מעשר מילים באנגלית. ספרתי.» → Coffee · July · Business · Lasagna · Australia/Melbourne
find it → read it off the board (**strip the context**, your handwriting, no logo) → stamp it in
«שמתי פה שש. מצאתן אחת-עשרה. את השאר לא שמתי אני — הן פשוט גרות פה.»
◆ They leave the sofa and hunt the house → that's your dissolve.

**Bodies** — fastest recovery when anything dies
"⟨name⟩ — jump! Both — give me the pencil!" Deadpan, no gesture leaks.
◈ Gap shows → hand the commands to the older.

**Two teachers**
«עכשיו שתיכן המורות שלי. כל אחת מלמדת אותי מילים שהשנייה לא אמרה.» Then mispronounce badly.
● How a correction between them lands = the together-or-separate decision.

**The notebook** — no pre-written page; the rule is shown, never announced
You write English in front of her → she answers **out loud** → you write the next line → hand it back.
`HI K!` → `I AM FROM AUSTRALIA.` → `HOW ARE YOU?` → `I LIKE PIZZA. WHAT DO YOU LIKE?` → `LOOK UNDER THE TABLE.`
Hebrew answer → look at the page, wait, shrug: «היא לא הבינה.»
▲ *"You're the one writing it"* → agree instantly, keep the mechanism. Never defend a fiction.

**It-Takes-Two** (~5 min)
«ספרי לי מה קורה שם — מי הדמות, מה היא רוצה?» → meaning / sound / nothing.
◈ Split verdict = strongest evidence for separate sessions.

## Dials — same words, different difficulty

Turn **one** at a time. Prefer far-temporal and far-social; they stay attached to real people.

- **time** — now → last year → next year
- **space** — this room → your city → a country you've never seen
- **who** — you → your sister → a stranger → someone who disagrees
- **real** — what is → what could be → what couldn't

## Traps — one or two, not six

Bait only what nobody could know. Never what you taught her.

1. All those languages say *ananas*. What's the English?
2. Spanish *sábado*, German *Samstag* — guess where those come from. (שבת)
3. «ראיתי את הכלב» — what does את mean in English? (nothing)
4. English names this bird after a country. Hebrew too — which? (הודו / they'll say Turkey)
5. Someone in Israel offers you צ'יפס — what do you get? (fries)
6. How do you say «אני מורה» properly? (*I teacher*)

**Stew:** call it easy → take the wrong guess, **don't correct it** → **«למה חשבת ככה?»** → more guesses, let them argue → follow what they open → **land it** → close it before you leave → board it.

**The landing:** a block she can already do, named before you bait. Bodies, stamps, board the column. A derail with no floor is a stall.
**Want a repair? Elicit, don't recast.** *"How would you say that again?"*
Stew longer with the twelve than the nine.
