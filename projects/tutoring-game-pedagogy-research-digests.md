# Word World pedagogy research — raw fleet digests (appendix)

Six research agents, 2026-08-23, launched at Idan's request before the pedagogy brainstorm. This file preserves their full digests with source links; the working synthesis is [[tutoring-game-pedagogy-research]]. Evidence labels are each agent's own (META / RCT / QUASI / THEORY / LORE and equivalents).

## Agent 1 — Game-based learning effectiveness

- Serious games beat conventional instruction: learning d=0.29, retention d=0.36; NOT more motivating d=0.26 ns; 77 studies (Wouters et al. 2013, J Ed Psych; META)
- Moderators: better when supplemented with other instruction, multiple sessions, group play (Wouters 2013; META)
- Digital games overall g=0.33 [0.19,0.48] k=57; design-within-medium matters more than medium (Clark, Tanner-Smith & Killingsworth 2016, RER; META)
- L2 vocabulary: d=0.986 across 26 studies (Chen, Tseng & Hsiao 2018, BJET; META)
- Adventure/contextual games d=1.867 vs other genres d=0.705 (Tsai & Tsai 2018, C&E; META)
- Mobile-game language learning g=0.962; single vs multiplayer ns (CALL journal 2025; META)
- Caveats: heterogeneity, small samples, publication bias, novelty; treat ~1.0 as upper bound, 0.3–0.5 safer (cross-meta)
- Instructional support in/around games adds d=0.34; selection-support d=0.46 (Wouters & van Oostendorp 2013; META)
- Debriefing after play aids transfer: thin quantitative base (Crookall 2014; THEORY/LORE)
- Intrinsic integration: content delivered through the core mechanic in the game's own representations (Habgood & Ainsworth 2011, JLS; THEORY+RCT). Zombie Division: intrinsic variant learned more at fixed 2h and chosen 7x longer free-choice (single RCT). Pre-registered replication n=210: effect real, mechanism is ATTENTION (players learn what the mechanic makes them process) (Cutting & Iacovides 2022; pre-registered replication)
- Concrete tests: (1) ignore test — can you win ignoring the knowledge? (2) strip test — remove content, still a game? (3) decision test — does winning USE the meaning? (4) representation test — content in the fantasy or in a bolted-on quiz screen?
- Feedback content: elaborated ES=0.49 vs correct-answer 0.32 vs right/wrong 0.05 (Van der Kleij 2015, RER; META)
- Feedback timing: overall null g=0.03, but immediate favored for primary age + retention (Ed Psych Rev 2026 META)
- Retrieval practice vs restudy g=0.50 (Rowland 2014) / g=0.61 (Adesope 2017) (META, robust)
- Retrieval beats copying for 5th-grade spelling at 4-day delay, ONLY with immediate corrective feedback (Jones et al. 2015; RCT)
- Spacing d=0.85 verbal material, 317 experiments (Cepeda 2006; META, robust)
- Guidance: unassisted discovery LOSES d=−0.38; guided discovery d=0.50; elicited self-explanation d=0.36 (Alfieri 2011; META)
- Pretraining: unsettled (Mayer meta 2025). Redundancy: contested for beginner L2 children, text+audio may aid decoding (Mayer meta 2025; META)
- Optimal training accuracy ~85% (error 15.87%) (Wilson et al. 2019, Nat Comms; computational THEORY)
- Copy-typing: copying word forms during initial learning IMPAIRED recall vs viewing (Barcroft 2006/2007, TOPRA; replicated lab series). With ample time + form-meaning attention, writing CAN help (Webb & Piasecki 2018; Jin & Webb 2025; mixed)
- Handwriting beats typing for young readers' letter/word-form learning; typing OK as retrieval/production medium (J Exp Child Psych 2025 etc.; RCT converging)
- Its top implications: recall-mode catches; spaced re-encounters; tutor stays in loop; elaborated error feedback with retype; run every mechanic through the four integration tests; meaning-conditional catches; worked example before each new frame; campfire debrief; ~85% per-girl tuning; co-op without leaderboards.

## Agent 2 — Incentives and motivation

- SDT needs (autonomy/competence/relatedness) predict enjoyment + future play (Ryan, Rigby & Przybylski 2006; survey+experiments). Causal 2x2: matching features raise matching need, mediate enjoyment/effort (Peng et al. 2012; RCT)
- Element mapping: badges/graphs→competence; avatars/story/teammates→relatedness (Sailer et al. 2017; RCT)
- Choice boosts IM/effort/performance; best 2–4 live choices (Patall 2008, 41 studies; META)
- Overjustification: expected tangible rewards undermine — engagement-contingent d=−0.40, completion d=−0.36, performance d=−0.28; unexpected/task-noncontingent: no effect. Verbal praise ENHANCES (d=+0.33); informational delivery d=+0.66 vs controlling d=−0.44. Tangible undermining STRONGER in children (Deci, Koestner & Ryan 1999; META). Cameron & Pierce contest magnitude; consensus: real for expected+tangible+contingent (2001 exchange; contested META)
- IM predicts performance ρ=.21–.45, k=183 (Cerasoli 2014; META)
- Task-congruent rewards preserve IM: book reward > token reward for reading motivation (Marinak & Gambrell 2008; RCT)
- Gamification: cognitive g=0.49, motivational g=0.36 (only cognitive survives high-rigor subsplit); game fiction + collaboration-with-team-competition are the moderators that matter (Sailer & Homner 2020; META). g=0.504 with 1–3 month sweet spot (Bai, Hew & Huang 2020; META)
- Decay: badges+leaderboard course DECLINED in IM/satisfaction over 16 weeks, lower exams mediated by lost IM (Hanus & Fox 2015; longitudinal QUASI)
- Streaks: commitment+loss aversion; no peer-reviewed learning benefit (industry LORE)
- Unlockables: thin direct literature; SDT reading = competence feedback + expanding autonomy; task-congruent informational class = lowest undermining risk (THEORY)
- Endowed progress: pre-seeded progress lifts completion 34% vs 19% (Nunes & Drèze 2006; field experiment). Loss-framing buys compliance, costs enjoyment (mixed experiments)
- Cooperative goal structures beat competitive d≈0.49 and individualistic d≈0.53; active ingredients positive interdependence + individual accountability (Johnson & Johnson; META)
- Cross-age tutoring g=0.34 overall; tutors gain g=0.39, tutees g=0.33 (Robinson et al. 2025; META)
- Upward comparison lowers self-concept; adult comparison between children damages self-esteem (Dijkstra 2008; review)
- Its top implications: protect world-changes-as-rewards; protect no-leaderboard; structure co-op as positive interdependence; D-teaches-K moments; 2–4 live choices; process praise; protect no-timers and zero-ceremony streak resets; lean into fiction; seeded progress bars with 2–3 tracks in reach; plan novelty refresh at 1–3 months.

## Agent 3 — Child L2 acquisition

- Incidental learning needs 6–20+ meetings; design target 8–12 contextual encounters per word (Rott/Horst/Webb/Elgort/Waring; converging RCT-lab)
- Repetition r=.41 with learning, ~17% variance; encounter quality matters (Uchihara, Webb & Yanagisawa 2019; META)
- Deliberate flashcard-style study fast + durable; balance with meaning-focused use (Nation & Webb; Four Strands ~25% each; THEORY+experiments)
- Spacing: spaced>massed medium-large, 48 experiments N=3,411; equal ≈ expanding schedules; distribute across days, shape secondary (Kim & Webb 2022; META)
- Successive relearning: 3 correct retrievals session 1 + relearn to criterion ~3 spaced sessions → 1-week recall 20%→80% (Rawson & Dunlosky; RCT series)
- Receptive precedes productive; gap persists without productive training; produce-from-meaning yields both directions (Webb 2005/2009; RCT-lab)
- Testing effect in children: g≈0.5–0.6, robust grades 1–6 when initial success high + feedback immediate; low-stakes retrieval REDUCES test anxiety (Rowland/Adesope/Agarwal; META)
- Input + output complementary (Krashen vs Swain synthesis); ages 8–13 oral/aural entry channel; establish oral vocabulary before decoding (Cambridge ELT; consensus)
- TPR: consistently positive for young-learner vocabulary; small unrandomized studies (QUASI)
- Dual coding/multimedia glosses positive; L1 gloss often equals or beats picture for beginners (META, mixed)
- Enactment effect: performing/observing congruent actions improves action-word recall; durable at 14 months (Macedonia; RCT-lab + longitudinal QUASI; 2023 review k=7: observing ≈ enacting, both beat audiovisual-only)
- COPYING a visible word is weak-to-NEGATIVE encoding (Barcroft 2003–2009 TOPRA; replicated). Retrieval beat word-writing at 1 week, n=179 Dutch EFL (Candry et al. 2020; RCT)
- Handwriting > keyboard for children's word-form/spelling learning (multiple 2025 RCTs; converging). Typing fine as retrieval medium
- Hebrew L1: phonology transfers, orthographic skill script-specific; /θ/, /w/-/v/, short vowels degrade spelling; English deep orthography needs explicit phonics (Abu-Rabia lineage; QUASI)
- Chunks: children abstract grammar from stored multiword frames; formulaic sequences processed faster; frames need varied fillers AND varied frames or they freeze (Tomasello; N. Ellis; THEORY strong base)
- YLE Pre-A1 Starters: ~500 words; present simple/continuous, can/can't, have got, there is/are, imperatives, question words, prepositions (official syllabus)
- Its verdict: copy-typing visible words is the WEAKEST mechanic; single highest-value change = type-from-memory with instant feedback (flash word, blank it, cue with picture/Hebrew).

## Agent 4 — Risks and externalities

- Screen time: low/moderate gaming (<2–3h/day) zero or slightly positive well-being association, n=120k (Przybylski & Weinstein 2017; strong correlational). Tech explains ~0.4% of adolescent well-being variance (Orben & Przybylski 2019; specification-curve). WHO under-5 limits rest on evidence WHO itself rated very low quality (critiques). Displacement is the mechanism: bedtime device use doubles inadequate-sleep odds (Carter 2016 JAMA Peds; META). This context: LOW risk; watch late-night solo play only
- IGD prevalence <1–2% higher-quality studies; over-gaming often symptom of underlying distress (Paulus 2018; contested definitions)
- Habit-risk features Word World LACKS: variable-ratio loot, ads, social pressure, infinite content, autoplay (design-ethics taxonomies)
- Gaming the system: exploiting mechanics to advance without learning; associates with worst outcomes of any disengaged behavior, predicts long-term outcomes (Baker 2004–2010 ITS series; strong replicated). Driven by disliking/fearing material, clusters on badly designed steps = design signal (Baker 2008 UMUAI). Detection + responsive agent + supplemental exercises halved gaming (Scooter the Tutor; moderate)
- Countermeasures: reward action = learning action; typed production over recognition; vary items; never reward speed alone. Word World residual exploits: memorized list order, sister dictating, copying visible text. REAL, MODERATE, addressable
- Overjustification spillover: real risk narrow here — if English exists ONLY in-game, words become game currency; keep non-game English alive. REAL BUT SMALL
- Streaks: loss-aversion converts record into obligation; broken-streak abandonment (what-the-hell effect); lenient streaks beat strict (industry A/B). Word World as built: LOW risk; never add calendar-day streaks
- Sibling comparison: parental "smarter sibling" belief predicts future GPA gap 0.21 independent of prior grades (Jensen & McHale 2015); upward comparison lowers perceived competence (BFLPE β≈−0.28); ability praise induces helplessness, process praise protects (Mueller & Dweck 1998; strong lab). 9yo sharing 12yo-tuned lists + fully visible hot-seat performance = the helplessness recipe. DESIGN-CRITICAL, top risk
- Gating: productive-failure advantage REVERSES for grades 2–5 (≈−0.09), young children need instruction first (Sinha & Kapur 2021 RER; META). Blocking the core verb (walking) is high-stakes gating; needs graceful-miss path. REAL, MODERATE
- Home use: errors practiced without corrective feedback entrench (fossilization, contested construct; corrective feedback d≈0.6, Li 2010 META); absent adult removes the talk that drove talk-share gains. REAL, MODERATE, addressable via logs + strict spelling
- Its top mitigations: separate sisters' progression surfaces; soften walk gate (hints after 2 misses); process-framed praise copy; anti-gaming pass (shuffle, full production, no speed rewards, attempt logs); home-play log reviewed weekly; strict spelling + immediate correct-form retype; within-session streaks only; keep non-game English; complementary co-op roles; ignore minutes, watch context.

## Agent 5 — Precedents

- Influent: object IS the flashcard, click anything to name it; n=8 study only. Criticism: isolated nouns, no production
- Noun Town VR: grey world regains color per word mastered; built-in SRS respawn. Reviews-only
- Crystallize (Cornell): harvest words from NPC dialogue, SPEND them assembling utterances; lab n=42 + wild n=186 significant gains; PAIRS learned more and enjoyed more (published, strongest in niche)
- Minecraft EE: language emerges from collaboration; small studies; silence creates space for learner talk
- Duolingo: company-commissioned efficacy (Vesselinov 2012, no control) + engagement-over-learning criticism; leagues punish non-competitive kids; recognition ≠ production. Steal: short sessions, generous immediate feedback
- Duolingo ABC: independent-but-funded eval +28% literacy in 9 weeks (irrelevant age band; credibility model)
- Memrise: learner-made mnemonic images. Drops: hard 5-min session cap creates appetite. Lingokids: exposure-heavy retrieval-light critique
- Martha Speaks (PBS): small fixed target set repeated across MANY activity contexts, +31% tested vocab (published moderate)
- ZType: first-keystroke target lock; word length = enemy HP; panic-valve EMP. Epistory: ONE input verb (typing) does combat/traversal/unlocks; invisible adaptive difficulty from accuracy+WPM. Textorcist: interruption pressure, boss-moments only. Nitro Type: racing rewards speed, technique fossilizes (cautionary)
- Quizlet Live: answers distributed across teammates' devices, cooperation mandatory (published moderate). Kahoot: motivation up, learning inconsistent; speed-scoring → frantic guessing (Wang & Tahir 2020 review). Wordwall: one list, ~20 mechanic skins. Baamboozle: luck cards keep weaker player in contention. Blooket: most engaging perceived, NO post-test difference (skins raise engagement only)
- CALL notables: DGBLL metas d≈0.5–1.0; adventure d=1.867 vs drill 0.705; ~5 spaced encounters durable; Kinect TPR gains (moderate)
- Prodigy Math (cautionary): math as interruption to combat; cosmetic economy outcompetes learning verb; "wander about shopping" even in funded research. Chocolate-covered broccoli test: remove the English — still a game? Then the English was broccoli
- Steal list: spaced respawn; harvest-then-spend; distributed answers; invisible adaptive difficulty; world-state progress (color/populate regions); fixed target set many skins; length-as-power; object-is-flashcard ambient labels; verb embodiment; luck-card mercy + panic valve; accuracy scores, never speed.

## Agent 6 — Assessment and progress

- Stealth assessment (Shute ECD): competency model + evidence model + task model; honest = declare map up front, fix scoring rules before data, validate once against external measure. Simplest defensible at n=2: per-word evidence table + fixed mastery rule + monthly external spot-check (THEORY + validated cases)
- Log predictors of recall (1.9M Duolingo traces): cumulative correct practice > lag since last practice > historical accuracy > error count; latency adds little; AUC .61 (Frontiers Educ 2026; large observational). Half-life regression: recall = 2^(−lag/half-life), half-life grows per correct exposure (Settles & Meeder ACL 2016)
- BKT/DKT unfittable at n=2; N-consecutive-correct heuristic is provably an optimal mastery policy under BKT variant — 3-in-a-row is legitimate (AIED 2020; proof)
- Fast ≠ learned; skip latency rules entirely (old weak study + motor confound)
- Testing effect LARGER at 1–6 day delay (g=.82) than same-day (g=.56) — weekly cadence ideal (Adesope 2017; META)
- Schedule: re-test W+1, W+3, W+7; equal ≈ expanding means missed slots are harmless (Kim & Webb 2022; META)
- Productive form-recall = the durable criterion; retained = produced correctly ≥1 week later, cold (Laufer & Goldstein lineage; consensus)
- YLE Starters task formats as fun checks: listen-and-color, picture yes/no, spell-the-pictured-word, describe-the-scene
- 85% rule default; kids below ~70% success mainly buy frustration; lower-ability children benefit from fewer errors (Wilson 2019 + small mixed child studies)
- Adaptive knobs in evidence order: review/new mix (~75/25), word length/frequency band, time pressure LAST (motor confound)
- Comparison praise curtails IM for GIRLS even during success; mastery praise enhances (Corpus, Ogle & Love-Geiger 2006; RCT-style). Normative feedback pushes performance-avoidance (Learning & Instruction 2017). Leaderboard harm conditional, public low rank hurts (Li 2024 review)
- Displays that work: cumulative non-decaying mastery artifacts (garden, word-tree), named milestones, personal bests. Displays that harm: rankings, decaying scores, percent-of-other-player
- Its top implications: weekly 5-word cold probe (no code); per-word evidence sheet (no code); W+1/3/7 steering (no code); session success-rate steering >90% harder <75% easier (no code); never compare the girls aloud; weighted respawn 75/25 biased to longest-unseen (small code); per-player mastery garden (small code); log probe results into save (small code); monthly YLE-style mini-games as external validation; typo-fail vs meaning-fail distinction (later).
