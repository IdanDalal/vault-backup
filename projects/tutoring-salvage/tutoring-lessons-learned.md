## 2. New lessons per session (deltas only)

### S1 (08-10, together, ~46 min) - `sessions/2026-08-10-K-D/10_Aug_at_9-10.LESSON.md`
- **One-to-many split = a target class.** One Hebrew word covering two English words (crab/lobster, L679-689); ship/sheep is the sound-side twin (L735-743). Loanword and translation shortcuts fail exactly there. NEW.
- **Cartoon anchor for disambiguation:** Mr. Krabs is a crab; "with claws like that he'd be a lobster" (L687-689). NEW.
- **Minimal pair taught through consequence:** "say 'I have a ship' and they hear a boat" (L740-743). Keep the stakes framing and drop the embarrassment framing (P7.5). NEW.
- **Opening thesis to retire:** "let's check which words you don't need to learn" (L47, L621). It set up the harvest-of-known-words problem on day one. NEW (root cause of C1).
- **Hunt drifts to label junk:** printed labels yield brands, place names and numbers (L296, L328). If items are planted, plant for frontier nouns. NEW.
- Referential opener ("what would you be doing this morning?", L7-37) gave the longest early turns. Already in vault (P7.2).

### S2 (08-13, sequential 1-on-1s) - `sessions/2026-08-13-K-D/13_Aug_at_9-00.LESSON.md`
- **The only real frontier word got cut.** Meme was unknown to D (L182); Idi vetoed it as "new and hard" (L207-208). Correction: the veto started with the tutor, and D followed at L435. Rule: log every unknown before pruning. NEW.
- **Forgot vs never knew:** muscle was forgotten (L381, "I forgot, but I know it"), not new. Ask "seen it before?": forgot = retrieval, never knew = teach. NEW (corrects the distillation).
- **Found words never reached the loop.** Kitchen finds (olive, oil, flour, toothpicks) were never said-then-written; dictation ran on known words only (L1101-1310). Write or photograph each find the moment it turns up. NEW.
- **Timebox discovery:** ~17 min of hunting for ~5 new items vs ~6 min of dictation with real corrections. Cap the hunt around 8 min. NEW.
- **Self-set difficulty:** K, "don't tell me" (L1011), "I want to remember it" (L1299). Give her the silence. NEW (student-triggered P7.1).
- **Blame the text:** "hard because it's Italian, the English is fine" (L865-871). The positive twin of the ban on "easy". NEW.
- **Age-calibrate props:** D (12), "stickers are over for me" (L678-679). NEW.
- **The student reads the plan:** D read the app's procedure aloud (L606-612), which gave English reading and buy-in at once. NEW.
- **Time said aloud hands over the clock:** "already at 45" (L538), and D closed her own turn. NEW.
- Tutor joined dictation as a player ("I'll try too", L1223-1224): the earliest instance of the co-player role behind C7. NEW (origin).

### S3 (08-17, K then D, then a hangout) - `sessions/2026-08-17-K-D/17_Aug_at_8-57.LESSON.md` (857) + `17_Aug_at_10-06.LESSON.md` (1006)

- **Accept one student-invented rule variant, timeboxed** (K's reverse game, 857:L1306). NEW.

## 3. Playbook for a new single student

Tags = evidence session. O = observed, I = inferred.

### Keep

| Item | Why | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Dictation loop: TTS or tutor says it, student says it, writes it on the whiteboard, checks | densest learning per minute | S2 O, vault P2 |
| Typing gate tied to her own choice ("you want a girl, type it") | choice turns into production at zero cost | S5 O L453 |
| Owned-object frames (This is ___ / It's ___ and ___) + transfer to a second object | real sentences, where random lists gave salad | S3 O |
| Word families from a root she owns (bounce/bouncy) | a new word usable the same day | S3 O |
| Target class: one L1 word = two English words, and minimal pairs with a consequence | where translation shortcuts fail | S1 O |
| Reading prompts inside her games ("can you read it?") x3 per session | cheap English inside free play | S5 O, S6 O |
| Input fields as typing gates (search, name, AI prompt boxes) | the only typing source in free play | S6 O |
| Her own artifacts as prompt material (drawings, collections) | highest-motivation English | S3 O, S6 O |
| Tutor as co-player, "you taught me" role reversal | talk share 73% to 46% | S2 I, S4 O, S5 O |
| Share your own habit target, let her police it | she owns the rule | S3 O |
| Credit the text for difficulty ("odd font", "Italian") | protects self-efficacy | S2 O |
| Honor "don't tell me": silence, let her try | self-set challenge = effort | S2 O |
| Re-ask a glossed word 5-10 min later as a question | one exposure lapsed in 7 min | S5 O |
| Refusals and errors as rewording puzzles | vocabulary from friction | S6 O |

### Drop

| Item | Evidence |
|---|---|
| "Let's find what you already know" as the session-1 frame | S1 O |
| Harvesting words from slang, labels and room objects without a probe | S1 O, S2 O, vault C1 |
| Hunts over ~8 min, or finds that never reach the write step | S2 O |
| Vetoing an unknown word without logging it | S2 O |
| Explaining the tutor's tools, AI or pipeline to the child | S3 O, S6 O |
| Testers framing ("tell me what you think of my game") during play | S5 O L32, L3872 |
| Free yes to "show me something else" with no price | S5 O |
| Admin, installs, accounts, file transfers inside session time | S5 O, S6 O |
| Untested AAA titles, or untested features pitched live | S5 O, S6 O |
| Tutor reading every UI string aloud | S6 O |
| Sessions over ~60 min without planned breaks | S4 O, S5 O |
| Stickers or kid rewards for students 12+ | S2 O |

### Pacing and rituals
- Put English in the moments of strongest interest, where D talked strategy in Hebrew for 20 min: ask for one-word English answers (left, right, wait, go). S5 O
- Offer a break by taking it, since an offer gets refused (S5 O). Put the break in the tool (C9).
- "Yes, after one more word" is the exit price at every request to switch. S5 I
- Pitch a new feature only while her hands are on the game, between wins. S5 O
- Say elapsed time aloud when you want her to own the close. S2 I
- Close with the counter update done by her (P6, vault).

### Warning signs (switch within 2 min)

| Signal | Evidence |
|---|---|
| She defines the word before you finish asking = item known, skip it | S2 O |
| "How long has it been?" around minute 14 | S3 O |
| Tutor opens with "the AI told me", then launches a multi-minute monologue | S3 O |
| Tutor notices the list "doesn't connect" | S3 O |
| Chatter about school or schedules just before an exit | S5 O |
| She names alternative games | S5 O |
| "Hard for me", "I'm not typing" | S5 O |
| Tutor says "nothing happens here" or "I don't know how" twice | S6 O |
| "Same as the last game" | S6 O |
| Body: hand pain, "I want to sleep", "scary" | S4 O, S5 O |
| A game asks for account or identity data: exit at once | S6 O |

### First-session checklist
1. Parent line before the session: recording consent, platform accounts, purchases, violent games, uploads of her photo to AI apps. S5 O, S6 O
2. Pre-play every game or tool; one control card; installs done. S5 O, S6 O
3. Frontier probe (P1), with an unknown-word log that pruning never deletes. S1 O, S2 O
4. Ask "seen it before?" on every miss. S2 O
5. One owned-object anchor chosen from her interests. S3 O
6. Dictation loop with the whiteboard, and finds written within the minute. S2 O
7. At least one typing gate tied to her choices. S5 O, S6 O
8. Three reading prompts planned inside whatever she plays. S5 O
9. Timer for breaks; session capped around 60 min. S4 O, S5 O
10. The counter seeded with 5-10 known words, updated by her at the close (P6). vault
11. Rationale in one sentence in your own voice, with the AI unmentioned. S3 O, S6 I

### Safety (any minor)
- Public servers: stranger invites, "press yes" to strangers' requests, entering strangers' houses (S6 L2264-2389). Private servers or offline modes only. S6 O
- Child photos went to two third-party AI apps, and the apps blocked them, not a rule (S6 L2758-2806, L3434-3437). Rule: no child photos to any upload service. S6 O
- Real-money prompts with a child at the keyboard (S5 L2804). S5 O
