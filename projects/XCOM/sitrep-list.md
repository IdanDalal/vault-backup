## PCS types in the loot tables (strike the ones to remove; tiers stay)

Stat sims come in Common / Advanced / Superior (Basic, Rare, Epic tables): Agility (dodge), Conditioning (HP), Focus (will), Perception (aim), Speed (mobility), Hacking, Psi (the last two from LW2 Abilities and Cut Content Psionics).
LW2 specials, one tier each: Combat Awareness (Basic: +15 defense and half armor on overwatch), Depth Perception (Basic: +5 aim, -25 enemy dodge from high ground), Emergency Life Support (Basic: first killing blow does not kill, longer bleed-out), Hyper-Reactive Pupils (Basic: +10 aim after a miss), Body Shield (Rare: target gets -20 aim, -50 crit against you), Iron Skin (Rare: melee damage -3), Smart Macrophages (Rare: faster wounds, acid and poison immunity), Combat Rush (Epic: kill buffs allies within 7 tiles), Damage Control (Epic: +2 armor for two turns after damage), Impact Fields (Epic: activate for 33% damage reduction two turns).
Removal is per template inside the three item tables that Odd S9 already redefines; jepFixes redefines them again, last loader wins.
