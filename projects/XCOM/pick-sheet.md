# Pick sheet: Choose My Class (100 offers)

Card = Primary - Secondary - Tertiary. 751 cards left in the game. Every one has all its guaranteed perks working (checked against the in-game weapon rules of every perk) and is the best its primary + secondary can get.

**The weapon line under each card lies sometimes**: it prints the first weapon each spec lists, which can belong to the other slot. Trust the class, then equip: Alien Hunter (label says boltcaster; it carries sword in the secondary slot); Phoenix (label says chemthrower; it carries canister in the secondary slot); Riot (label says shotgun; it carries shield in the secondary slot); SwordMaster (label says sword; it carries shield or sword in the secondary slot).

## 3. Tie-break

- More specs you have not run yet > perfect > good > filler.
- At every later promotion: a perk whose yellow weapon list names nothing the soldier carries is deleted in missions. Pick another (201 of the cards have at least one such optional perk; guaranteed perks are all fine).
