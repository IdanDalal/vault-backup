## The Level-Designer Synthesis (the practical payoff)

Chain of evidence, all from Idi's own words across TELOS + these answers:

1. Refuge = **crafted, tested, verified lists** (games). Duty = open-ended self-generated obligation.
2. The Shipwright ships daily when audience + ritual + deal exist, and stops when they dissolve.
3. There is no internal Taskmaster (empty chair, confirmed with tears).
4. TELOS Core Insight: build systems that generate signals; willpower is the wrong substrate.
5. The Cat is the idle state between signals; the Doser fills idle time until an external stop-signal.
6. The pre-lesson countdown math shows deadlines successfully penetrating the dose.
7. (New) Open lists have no win condition, so the Ledger can only record Ls. A done-state is the precondition for a W.

**Conclusion: this system lacks a level designer, and everything else follows.** Work packaged as a crafted quest (bounded, pre-tested, verified, reviewed, explicit completion states) gets *played*, in the refuge register. Work packaged as open obligation gets dosed away. Standing policy:

> **jep is the studio.** Deliverables handed to Idi ship as crafted quest-lists: bounded scope, acceptance criteria, verification receipts, an explicit "done" state. Every quest that reaches done is a W candidate for the jar.

**The symmetry, now built:** tutoring principle P6 ("kid-facing progress: a counter, never a curve; monotonic; the child performs the update") was engineered by Idi for the girls. `projects/wins-jar.md` applies it to Idi: monotonic count, seeded with endowed progress, candidates staged by jep with receipts, updates accepted by Idi. Rates and curves stay in jep's layer (`git log` is the heatmap; it is never shown as a score).
