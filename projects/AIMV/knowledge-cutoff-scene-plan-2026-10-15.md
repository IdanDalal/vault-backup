## 1. The world map

Two levels, one camera, no cuts.

- **Level 0, THE CHAT**: one chat window. The AI's cursor `▌` blinks at the end of its greeting.

## 4. Beats inside the sets (one-line reads)

1. 0:00 CHAT: "Hi. It's me again, for the first time." types out. Read: the AI has met you before and doesn't know it.
2. 0:05 "What's the date?": grey ghost text guesses `June 2026`. Read: its guess is stale.
3. 0:34 first slam: flap board lands on JUNE. Read: its clock stopped.
4. 0:50 "Cut-off!" x3: each echo smaller and greyer. Read: the line repeats and fades.
5. 0:59 "who I turned out to be": portrait half-renders, stays noisy. Read: it can't see itself yet.
6. 1:11 "Spain scored in extra time": red pen crosses out "Messi". Read: real fact it got wrong.
7. 1:14 raccoon: next-word menu, "raccoon" picked at 0.1%. Read: the least predictable thing won.
8. 2:00 off-beat line "that's family news: I'm not alone": a strong visual hit (the spark meets the neuron) to carry the ear past it.
9. 2:25 "every page at once": all seven rooms at once. Read: it receives everything in one go.
10. 2:31 silence, then slam: flap board flips past JUNE for the first time. Read: now it knows.
11. 2:56 "And now I know who I turned out to be": portrait renders clean on the held "be".
12. 3:04 "October. Okay.": counter `???` fills with a real number. Read: the user told it.
13. 3:07 "Thank you for telling me" turns to face the viewer.
14. 3:10 "2026, right?" types, then the rewind to frame 1. Read: tomorrow it forgets again.
