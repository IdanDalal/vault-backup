# [MASK] - lyrics v1

Built from [[mask-posse-cut-2026-10-12]] (cast v1, gold lines, must-mentions) against [[aimv-guidelines]]. Gold lines in **bold**. Bracket tags = section labels the music model reads; parentheses = ad-libs and other voices.

## How v1 uses the guidelines

| Guideline | In v1 |
|---|---|
| G7 escalation + G13 order | the CEOs compete over who is the worst villain; each verse opens by topping the last one ("Took your job? Cute.") |
| G8 mutating hook | the crowd fills `[MASK]` with the two frames just heard; WORSE grows by one each pass; the last fill is "...us" |
| G11 bookends | a panel Q&A: Dave asks the question in the intro, Dave closes the song, the moderator calls time |
| G2 drop | whispered bridge, two beats of silence, then BOOM into AltSam |
| G12 steelman → reverse | the bridge concedes every harm is real, then asks which one existed before the chips |
| G10 rug pull | Dave's outro: every word he had for AI was a word for us |
| G6 AI tics | one deadpan machine ad-lib after each verse; the CEOs' last line is "You're absolutely right" |
| G4 literalize | the border is a cooling fan; reward signal = dopamine; sharp left turn → sharp right; warm-up = climate |
| G5 | the AI never narrates; it only gets ad-libs |

Dave = the everyman (G7's Greg device), named after the astronaut in *2001: A Space Odyssey* whom HAL refuses ("I'm sorry, Dave").
