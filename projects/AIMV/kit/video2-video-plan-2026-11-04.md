# "Pay Attention": the video plan (proposal v1)

Proposal for Idi's word; nothing built. Inputs: song of record A-7003 4:53 (R23); the 2026-11-04 reference wave (9 unanalyzed videos watched, 10 analyzed refs + KC re-read, 402 technique cards in [[kit-techniques]]); Idi's visual taste (102 lines, `D:\work\aimv-lab\pa-video\refs\R-reader.md` section 2); KC's measured bar (same file, section 3). Times below = Whisper word starts in A-7003 (about 0.2 s early); forced alignment becomes the timing of record in phase 0.

## 1. The idea in one line

**Attention is light.** One lamp on a kitchen table at night is the only warm light in the video; every question she asks adds light, every pasted answer is cold screen-blue, and the viewer's own five minutes run down on a meter that ends at 0:00 on the last frame, exactly 5:00.00 in, with "Go on. Ask it." waiting in the composer.

Why this one: it carries the thesis (R16: asking questions = paying attention to life) as a picture rule that every frame can obey; it closes KC's two unmet camera targets with one set (gaps G1 continuous flight, G25 a travelling subject); it pays the viewer back for the attention they spent (R16's "acknowledge and reward them in a satisfying meta-contextual way").

3. 137 words/min with dense pictures: KC's reading-time rule (0.25 s per word + 0.5 s) and one hero word per frame.
4. The meter is a screen-stuck element, the kind Idi asked to minimize (taste #72); one element only, ruled in Q2.
