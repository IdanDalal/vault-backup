## 1. The idea in one line

**Attention is light.** One lamp-yellow point of light in a dark field of type is the only warm light in the video (R1's orange spark and R3's pink dot both carry the eye this way); every question she asks adds light, every pasted answer is cold screen-blue, and the viewer's own five minutes run down on a meter that ends at 0:00 exactly 5:00.00 after "Hi.", with "Go on. Ask it." waiting in the composer.

Why this one: it carries the thesis (R16: asking questions = paying attention to life) as a picture rule that every frame can obey; one travelling light gives the camera its reason across the whole song (gaps G1 continuous flight, G25 a travelling subject: KC's two unmet camera targets); it pays the viewer back for the attention they spent (R16's "acknowledge and reward them in a satisfying meta-contextual way").
