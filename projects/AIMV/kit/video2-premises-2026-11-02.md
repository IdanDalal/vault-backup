# Video 2: three premise candidates (jep drafts; Idi rules)

Built from: [[research-lyric-craft-2026-11-02]] (15 shared moves of the 6 lyric-bar songs), [[research-sound-craft-2026-11-02]] (7 sound rules of the 9), [[kit-lexicon]] open ground (wide terms no ref song touches), [[kit-atlas]], Idi's R14 (the leap is the song). People in the bridges are invented; no tutoring data is used anywhere.

Every candidate carries the bar's skeleton: stock-phrase title with a second life (move 1); one idiom field swept line by line (2); concede then turn (3); a bridge zoom to one private person at a dated moment with the band stripped (4); a planted object paid off late (7); the AI stating a limit at the peak, then offering what it can (6); outro restarting the loop a generation on (8); ~5 min, 15-18 sections, ~800 words, a fast section before the bridge (section 10). Sound: valleys of 6-15 dB with drums and bass out, a rising last third, two stop-and-slam silences, a pumping sub-bass that leaves between choruses (sound R1-R4).

## Recommendation: B, "No Pain, No Gain"

1. **Most roles per element.** The gym field maps one to one onto training (weights, reps as epochs, plateau, warm-up schedules, cool-down, bulking as scaling, cutting as distillation), and several of those terms are the lexicon's widest (scaling 11, weights 10, training 10, loss 9).
2. **The sound bar is the genre's native grammar.** Escalation, a pumping sub-bass, drop sets and a final max-out are what Idi named in It Crossed The Line, Not My Fault and Tell Me No (R14: the leap is the song).
3. **The widest register** (comic scaling bros → a man taking eleven steps → a grandson's practice) matches the move that scored highest in Idi's notes (Final Boss, "WILD").
4. **Picture-ready.** Plates, bars, curves and steps are objects the KC engine's type kits can build, and Accept All's arrow-on-a-track becomes the walk.

A is the strongest fit for TELOS and the most personal; C has the richest music-native wordplay and the cleanest moral peak. Any of the three can borrow the others' best lines.
