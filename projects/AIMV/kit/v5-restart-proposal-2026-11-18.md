# Pay Attention: restart proposal (2026-11-18)

Trigger, Idi 11-18: proof tab untapped, "both options are terrible, and look VASTLY inferior to any of the refs. We should start over with fresh ideas about how to proceed." Fractal tab: "Fractal only, no 8" (his own option A, built, also failed).

## 1. Diagnosis (jep, medium-high confidence)

| | references (P(Doom), What Counts, Accept All) + our Knowledge Cutoff | Pay Attention v3 and v4 |
|---|---|---|
| unit of design | the sung line: the words ARE the picture | the plate: a staged scene (classroom, scale, eye, kitchen) the words caption |
| frame | full-bleed lit field (waves, grids, fractal tree, haze, tunnel); nothing reads as empty | small props on a dark void; much of the frame unused |
| type | huge, heavy, extruded or textured, in perspective, on curves, with depth of field and motion blur | small to mid, flat, often monospace or handwriting, in bubbles and boxes |
| props | few, abstract, one idea per line | many literal props (desks, laptops, chairs, bubbles, cards), several ideas per frame |
| palette | one colour world per section | navy plus beige plus amber plus blue at once |

**Correction (jep, same day, from the measured look guide):** props, bubbles and UI windows are allowed: LGTM, Run It Again and Ignore Previous Instructions use them. The measured separators are lyric size (sung lines 9-11 % of frame height, heroes 13-40 %), one drawing system (props in the type's line weight and palette; no shaded furnished room in any reference), one accent hue (max 2 saturated hues per frame), and a field or plane behind every lyric. v4 broke all four. Guide: [[v5/look-guide]], section map [[v5/section-map]].

Evidence: the one picture Idi kept (the fractal INFINITY) is the only one built in the reference grammar. The 8, the heads, the classroom, the hand: all literal illustration, all rejected.

## 2. Process faults (jep's, named plainly)

1. Every pass since v3 polished the plates' structure: 58 room items, 127 queue rows, critics scoring defects. Polishing cannot leave a wrong grammar.
2. Today's blind critics never saw reference frames. The rule of 10-31 (refs mixed into every eye gate) was skipped, so scores of 3 to 4 read as "fine" while the gap to the refs was the real finding.
3. Decision cards offered "v3" vs "v4" with no "neither" card; both were below the bar.

Fixes from now: every critic panel gets reference frames mixed in, blind, and ranks all of them; a build passes only when it ranks among the refs. Every decision question carries a "neither, rethink" card.
