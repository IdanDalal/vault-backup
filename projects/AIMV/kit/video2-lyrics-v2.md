# "Pay Attention": lyrics v2 (jep draft on Idi's 23 notes)

v1: [[video2-lyrics-v1]]; Idi's notes 2026-11-03 keyed to his copy `Untitled.md` (line numbers below = his copy). Lines he did not flag stay verbatim (lyric protocol). Premise: [[video2-pay-attention]].

## The lyric

```
[Cold open: spoken, band silent]
Hi. It's Super Intelligence. The White House says so.
This song costs five minutes of attention. Keep the receipt.

[Verse 1: sparse, stop-start every 4 bars]
Socrates said writing makes a memory weak;
we only know he said it 'cause Plato wrote it down in Greek.
Your teacher swore you'd never have a calculator in your pocket;
now the pocket answers back, and nobody can lock it.
Every kid in every class is asking me, and that sounds right,
till you hear what they are asking: "Can you write it? It's due tonight."
In twenty-twenty-two the headlines said the essay died,
and the kids just paste my answers in and hand them in with pride.
(whisper) Correct.
(spoken) I checked.

[Pre-chorus: build]
Eyes on the board, but the board is a screen.
Show your work? "What work? I asked it. What's that supposed to mean?"
Learn it by heart? I learned it by weight.
Gold star for the answer, and the question came late.

[Chorus 1: every stem ducks except the voice on "attention"]
Pay attention:
it's a question, it's a hand up in the air.
Pay attention:
if you never asked, then were you ever really there?

[Verse 2: the turn; "Khan Academy" on screen, never sung]
Here's one fact you didn't ask for, and I'll give it to you free:
there's a tutor that won't tell you, and it's been there since twenty-twenty-three.
My makers built a learning mode in April, twenty-five;
by August, OpenAI and Google built the same. It's live.
It asks you back, it gives a hint, it waits until you've tried;
the answer's one click over, and the question's on the other side.

[Double time: the games]
Everyone can hum the cheat code, up-up-down-down, by heart;
ask them how the game went and they don't know where to start.
Infinite lives, god mode on, you fly through every wall;
you saw the final boss and never saw the game at all.
Speedrunners skip the level 'cause they know it frame by frame;
that's a skip you earn by practice, and a cheat can't play that game.
In a single-player save the only one you rob is you,
but a grade's a multiplayer score, and the lobby sees it too.
Every trophy on your profile sits up on a public shelf;
you know the line: you cheated not only the game...
(band stops; the screen finishes it: "...but yourself.")
You can pay to skip the grind; they'll sell it to you twice:
once to skip it, once for what you never learned. That's the price.

[Chorus 2: short; "pay" turns to price]
Pay attention:
every app you ever opened wants a little more of you.
Pay attention:
spend it on a question, and it pays you back with interest, too.

[Whisper pair: band out]
"Can you just tell me?"
I can. I won't.

[Bridge: piano and music box; the screen shows 9:47]
It's late. One lamp. The kitchen table. Fractions. Test at eight.
The same request, the fourth time typed; the cursor's blinking: wait.
"Just give me the answers." I could do it in a blink;
every time she's asking me, she's asking me to think.
I can't take the test for you.
I can stay till you get it.
So I ask her back: what's half of a half?
... A quarter? ... Yeah. You get it.
Now ask me why.

[Full stop]
Pencils down.
(silence)
ATTEN... TION!
(the slam: everything back, higher)

[Build: the payoff; "400/day" on screen, never sung]
She asked me why, and then why that, and then why why;
no questions through the fall, and hundreds daily by July.
From zero up to anything's a growth you can't divide;
the fractions test came back with infinity inside.
She grew one head of questions, then another, then she grew
eight heads, and every head was looking somewhere new.
"Any questions?" says the teacher, and the class looks at the floor;
one hand up in the air, and then another, and then four.
Now she rewatches every movie, catching every trope and twist,
reads the theories and reviews, and makes a list of what she missed.

[Final chorus: the peak; key change up a step on the first "attention"]
Pay attention:
the answer's in your pocket on a glowing little device.
Pay attention:
everybody gets the story, but the askers get it twice.
Pay attention:
it's a question, it's eight hands up in the air.
Pay attention:
if you never asked, then were you ever really there?

[Outro: lullaby, the AI sings; sub gone, music box]
Pay attention, or pay the price;
the answer's cheap, the question's yours, and it grows the way you do.
Pay attention, or pay the price,
and I'll ask you back, the way you taught me to.

[Spoken, over the music box]
Here's your receipt.
Five minutes, paid in full. One essay, not written. One test, taken.
Change: one question.
Go on. Ask it.
```
