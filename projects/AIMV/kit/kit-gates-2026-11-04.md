- Measures: integrated LUFS, LRA, true peak (ffmpeg ebur128, BS.1770), sample peak, full-scale samples, clipped runs (3+ samples at full scale), overs above -1 dBTP (own 4x oversampling).

## 2. Chance baseline (`chance.py`)

- Chance = share of frames within +-k frames of a grid point (what random cut times would score). Exact coverage and a 2,000-trial simulation agree (22 % for KC, 60 fps, +-2 frames).
- avsync already prints a half-beat null on its whole-video table; its loud/quiet, per-plate and accept tables printed on-beat rates bare. `chance.py --accept accept-X.json` adds the column.

| KC final render `full7b` (10-28) | baseline `full2` | final | chance | lift |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| graphic hits on beat +-2f | 29 % | 61 % | 22 % | 2.8x |
| graphic hits on beat +-1f | 20 % | 52 % | 13 % | 3.9x |
| graphic hits on downbeat +-2f | 10 % | 33 % | 6 % | 5.8x |
| cuts on beat +-2f | 50 % | 64 % | 22 % | 2.9x |
| cuts on downbeat +-2f | 16 % | 20 % | 6 % | 3.4x |

- `full2` graphic hits sat near chance (29 % vs 22 %); the rhythm pass (10-19) made the lock real. The 45 % target sat 2x above chance.
- Refs (avsync cache, `out\chance-avsync.md`): R4 cuts and hits at chance (every p >= 0.13); R2, R3, R5 graphic hits beat-locked (whole video, +-2f, p <= 0.001). R4 is a bar reference, so beat lock is one style among several.

## 3. Glitch gate (`flash.py`)

- Rule: a frame (or two) jumps away from its neighbours by more than max(2, 4x the local median step) and the picture comes straight back. Cuts never trip it (they do not come back).
- Planted test (KC 20 s, 60 fps): red frame, splice frame, 2-frame stray, double exposure: 4/4 caught; the double exposure moved 4.7 against a median step of 1.67; 0 false alarms in 1,196 clean frames.
- KC upload (4K, 11,646 frames, incl. the 11-01 repaired join): 0 flashes. Engine `flips.ts` (>= 60/255 brightness jumps) stays; this adds any short-lived change.

## 4. Animatic gate (`animatic.py`)

- What Idi sees: the song; the current lyric line lit word by word (sung white, now yellow, coming grey); the section tag with its direction from his final lyric; bar.beat; a scrolling strip with sections, loudness, beats and bars, every word, holds (blue), stops (red); a PICTURE box (empty until line cards exist, then `--plan`).
- Word times: Whisper words matched to the written lyric (difflib), gaps interpolated. Whisper runs about +-0.2 s on sung words: right for an animatic, never the timing of record (step 6 stays).
- Rendered: take 5153 (shortlist 1, 4:41 file) → `D:\work\AIMVs\ours\pa-final\animatic\pa-5153-animatic.mp4` (281.3 s, 1280x720, 30 fps, AAC) + `pa-5153-animatic.timemap.md` (84 lines, 17 sections with start/end, 14 holds, 29 stops, 691 beats at 143.6 BPM vs caption 144). - Known limits: bar numbers count from librosa's first beat, so they run during the band-silent cold open; lead-in shows the next line 0.6 s early once the current line's last word ends (Whisper word ends can sit before the sung hold ends).
