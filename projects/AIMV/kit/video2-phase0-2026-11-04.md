## 2b. Correction after Idi's approval (R26, 2026-11-04): v2 to v3

Idi watched the v2 animatic and ruled "the timing feels right". Building the engine data, jep found v2's "Socrates" (the first word of verse 1) at 6.52 s: Whisper never heard it, `align_take.py` interpolated it right after "receipt", and the line-start rule kept the guess; it is sung at 9.47 s, so v2 lit it about 3 s early.

- Search: 30 words where a confident forced alignment (score 0.5+) disagrees with v2 by more than 0.5 s (`pa\sync\suspects.py`).
- Decision per word by the vocal stem (`pa\sync\fix_suspects.py`): the candidate with the bigger energy rise across its start wins (0-120 ms after vs 120-0 ms before); FA when its rise beats v2's by 0.3+, else the strongest onset in the gap between the neighbours (after the previous word's end, 0.1 s before the next word) when it beats v2's by 0.5+.
- Result: 16 of 30 moved, 14 kept (table `pa\sync\fixes.md`). Biggest: "Socrates" 6.52 to 9.47; "She asked me why" 207.64 to 208.24 (the voice returns after "Pencils down."); "One essay" 285.04 to 286.07; "Now ask me why" 194.40 to 195.53; "or pay the price" 262.41 to 263.33; "Once" 142.38 to 142.97.
- jep miss inside the fix: the first fix pass searched from the previous word's start and put "She" inside "down." (207.30); caught by looking at the vocal energy (207.25-207.60 = "down.", silence to 208.25), corrected before any delivery.
- Timing of record = `pa\sync\pa-timing-v3.json`; click reel, both animatics and the engine data are rebuilt from v3.
