## Games: does the cheat-code comparison hold?

Holds:
1. Single-player cheats take only from the cheater: god mode removes the struggle the game was built to give, and the fun goes with it. Answer-pasting removes the struggle homework was built to give, and the learning goes with it.
2. Mods versus cheats maps to tutor versus answer key: a mod reshapes the game to fit the player (difficulty, hints, a tutorial); a cheat skips it. Learning modes are mods.
3. Speedrunners skip content through mastery: the one skip that proves skill. "Speedrun the lesson, never skip it."

Breaks (state these, or a critic will):
1. A lot of game grind is padding built for retention or to sell a skip; most practice in school is the learning itself (some is padding too).
2. Games are the goal; school skills get used later, outside the classroom, so the cost of skipping arrives years late and unseen.
3. Tests are multiplayer: a grade is a signal to other people (schools, employers), so exam cheating sits closer to an aimbot in online play, which harms others and gets banned.

Lyric use: verse 5 runs the holds, the bridge pays the breaks ("you can't respawn a year").
Cheat-code facts (Konami code, Doom's god mode IDDQD, noclip) are from memory: check each before a lyric uses it.
