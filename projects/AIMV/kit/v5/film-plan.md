# Pay Attention v5: film plan (Idi's 4 points, 2026-11-18)

Source: a YouTube comment on LGTM Idi quoted, and his notes. His thesis for this film: information density with satisfying returns on demanding attention; "paying attention pays off". That is the song's own title, so the film's method and its message are one thing.

Jokes and sarcasm: carried by objects and labels that say the dry true thing (the AI's own HUD, receipts, price tags, stats), never by captions explaining the joke.

## 2. Easter eggs and information density (the reward for watching)

Rules:
1. Every section carries 3-6 readable-on-pause details: true labels, real numbers, callbacks. A viewer who pauses always finds something; a viewer who doesn't is never blocked.
2. Only true, sourced facts or the AI's in-world voice (craft rule 9). Each fact verified with a source before it is built.
3. Callbacks across the film: the HUD `context N tokens` counts every word sung so far, continuously, so the number in chorus 3 is the film's real word count; attention arcs in later choruses reach back to tokens from earlier verses; the meter is one clock across all plates.
4. A new detail appears at least every ~4 s (the motion guide will set the measured rate).

Candidate facts (to verify before use): Phaedrus 275a (writing and memory); the "you won't always have a calculator in your pocket" line; The Atlantic "The College Essay Is Dead" (Dec 2022); Khanmigo (Khan Academy, 2023) for "a tutor that won't let you cheat"; Claude learning mode (Apr 2025), ChatGPT study mode and Gemini guided learning (summer 2025) for "my makers built a learning mode... by August"; the Konami code (up up down down left right left right B A) for "the cheat code"; "Attention Is All You Need" (2017, 8 heads) for the choruses and "eight heads".
