# Imaginative Prompting's method, read for Pay Attention

Researcher r-ip for jep, 2026-11-05. Everything below was read from his repos, his public WIP catalog and his tutorial transcript, unless marked `(inferred)`. Local copies: `D:\work\aimv-lab\pa-video\research\src\` (read only; no code from them was run).

Verdict: his method transfers almost whole. The look does not. What we lack most: a per-note checklist tied to frames, measured camera balance against his three films, a shot card per move, and flicker/cut detectors with planted failures.

Terms used once below: **blocking** = a pass with flat stand-in shapes that fixes camera, framing and timing before any detail; **animatic** = a blocking render played with the song; **stems** = the song split into separate instrument tracks; **fw/s** = frame widths per second, how fast the picture moves on screen; **waypoint** = any frame a camera move lands on or passes its key moment through.

## 2. His pipeline, numbered

1. **Vision first** (S5 0:33): a North Star and its themes; details may stay open. Ask Claude questions; ask Claude to ask you questions.
2. **Song**: Claude drafts lyrics to his story, he steers with topic picks (FelonyBench, "SI"); regenerate in Suno where a line sings badly; 10 takes for The End Of The Show (S5 7:05-7:37).
3. **Audio map before anything is keyed** (S1 song-timing): Suno's own stems + MIDI; every word's onset measured on the vocal stem twice independently (agree within ~25 ms); beat grid fitted to drum-stem audio onsets; kicks/snares/hats/crashes classified by band; one file of every word (onset, end, confidence, reason). A/B listening clips when a call is perceptual ("Listen: where do these words start? (breath or voice)", S4 ICMA 2026-10-04 13:49).
4. **Characters and the world's look**: one standout feature per character (blue beanie; S5 8:44); look options as complete looks (4 looks x day/night on the same room, S4 ICMA 10-04 00:03); a style sheet for the recurring symbol (the AI: palette hexes, where it appears section by section, a Never list; S4 WILAY "The AI: its style sheet"). Character pipeline (pelican, S4 ICMA 10-14): grey blockout in the shot, design check, 8 silhouettes judged at feed size, 2D pose sheet on the beats, 3D face study, body + pose sheet, turnaround, blocked on the song in two timings (A or B), placed in the shot as stills, motion drafts d1-d6.
5. **Section plan for his critique** (S4 WILAY "Verse 1: the plan"): approach, structure in song seconds, a world with his pick plus 2 alternatives, beat by beat (camera, scene, type per word with times), what it keeps, "Your calls" (3 numbered), and what happens after his OK. Nothing built before the OK.
6. **Lyric concepts for every line** (S4 WILAY "Lyric concepts, every line"): a table per section, time / line / concept; the type's material carries the meaning (cold steel = fear, warm felt = the people and the dog, the AI's light falls on words and words never glow).
7. **Storyboard stills, one frame per impact point, before any motion** (S4 ICMA "Bridge draft 6 storyboard", "Verse 3 storyboard 1").
8. **Blocking / grey animatic on the real song** (S1 blocking-first; S4 WILAY "Chorus 1: the grey animatic"): real camera, real word onsets, flat stand-ins in a colour code, renders in seconds at 720p muted with the WAV muxed; one shot per round, the creator describes each shot first. The blocking is data (keys per channel, C2-smooth monotone interpolation) read through pure functions `cameraAt(f)`, `objectAt(id,f)`.
9. **Scene spec on sign-off, then a fresh session per scene** builds the look on top of the frozen paths, importing them read-only (S1 scene-spec.md; S5 13:39-14:11).
10. **Camera plan v2 as a shot table to veto** when a draft feels slow (S4 ICMA "Chorus 2: camera plan v2"): 19 rows, time / words / move / scale change / speed now and planned, with measured speeds of a section he liked as the target.
11. **Drafts**: every pass a new entry with known issues on top, what changed, what to look at, the media, a link to the previous pass (S1 review-page; 208 "Known issues" blocks in S4).
12. **Director session** over up to 3 workers: relays his exact words plus routing facts, owns the GPU queue and schedule, reviews every render before he sees it, a recurring tick (30 min by day, 15 overnight) (S1 directing-sessions; S5 17:26-17:59).
13. **Render checks** on every render: blank, flicker, pop, cut, jitter, clipping; checks proven able to fail (S1 render-checks).
14. **Finals**: chunks with 12-frame pre-roll, ProRes joins re-encoded once, BT.709 stamped, WAV muxed separately, 4K stills compared with 1080p (S1 rendering).
15. **Release**: thumbnail from film assets, one subject ~1/3 frame readable at 168 px, 0-4 words, clear of the timestamp zone (right 22 % x bottom 20 %), checked at 360/168/120 px, YouTube Test & Compare (S4 WILAY "Thumbnail options"); a 9:16 Short rendered natively from the film's scenes with a mock Shorts UI over it (S4 ICMA "Chorus 3 Short: early look").

Scale receipts (S5 3:18): ~500 prompts per video, 700+ for Everybody's Got A Home; across 4 videos 100 B tokens read, 250 M written, ~98 % cached; 1 to 1.5 weekly usages of the 20x plan per video, his figure $50-75 per video.

## 4. The 10 highest-value changes to our kit, ranked

1. **Note checklist per plate, tied to frames.** Table: Idi's note verbatim (with time) → the frame that shows it met → the render it was checked on. Any change to the plate (camera, staging, timing) unchecks every row. Relays carry his exact words plus routing facts. Add the word-to-number table (S1 shot-design) for his size and position words. Changes: `pa-video\briefs\plates-common.md` Deliverables (a report section), `critic-template.md` (the critic also receives the checklist rows), lead procedure in `handoff-2026-11-05-pay-attention-phase4.md` §2. First use: chorus1 fix3, still pending his eye.
2. **Camera balance and smoothness probe over the joined cut.** New tool `pa-video\tools\camprobe.*`: per frame picture speed in fw/s (5x3 point grid projected frame to frame, or from the engine's camera functions where they exist), still share, moves per 10 s, landing share, hold lengths, turn rate, image speed, parameter reversals, jitter. Bands: still 30-40 %, 4.9-6.9 moves/10 s, 18-35 % landing, quiet verses, a still stretch near the end. Run on `pa-wip-0m00-1m21.mp4` now and on every wave. Step: `gates-window.sh post`, then the whole-video pass before Idi watches.
3. **A shot card per move in the wave 5 briefs.** One sentence: "From [start frame], [move] so we [reveal / follow / feel] [what], landing on [end frame] on [word or beat]"; if the only verb is "show", rewrite. A variety table per plate: neighbours differ in 2+ of size, height, side, lens, place. Changes: `plate-chorus3.md`, `plate-outro.md` (to write), and the card template in [[kit-line-cards]] (camera field) for video 3.
4. **Flicker, cut and jitter scans.** Flicker = a 1-3 frame change that undoes itself (the R31 rule 1 failure our critics keep finding: "fades out in 2-3 frames"); cut = histogram jump, then assert each cut sits on a beat and in the allowed-cut list (R31 rule 3); jitter = wander-and-return. His Python cores are in S1 render-checks. Change: `gates-window.sh post`, [[kit-gates-2026-11-04]].
5. **Every gate proven able to fail.** Plant one known-bad per gate (a popped word, a 2-frame tag, a screen-stuck box, an off-beat cut) and require a FAIL; log frames compared against frames expected. Change: [[kit-gates-2026-11-04]], `gates-window.sh` (a `--plant` mode).
6. **Known issues first in every handoff to Idi.** Each watchable: title, time added, known issues with song times on top, what changed, what to look at, link to the previous pass, a new filename per version; his reaction logged as "reviewed" with time, kept separate from "accepted". Change: the handoff template and `D:\work\AIMVs\ours\pa-song\proof\` naming. WIP (S2) as the page is a separate ruling for Idi.
7. **Blocking first for video 3.** Grey animatic of the whole song with the real camera and word onsets, flat colour-coded stand-ins, 720p muted plus WAV; camera as data read through `cameraAt(f)`, which detailed plates import read-only; storyboard stills one frame per impact point before motion; a scene spec on sign-off. Change: [[kit-process]] pipeline, [[kit-next-video]]. For waves 4-5: stills per impact point before the first video render.
8. **Impact grammar for slammed words.** Keep the on-beat slam; add a contact frame, compression and one damped rebound (zero-slope settle curve, S1 §2), and a response in what it hits (desk, chalk, the lamp's pool). Linear motion only for the machine's voice. Changes: `plates-common.md` lessons; `critic-template.md` "by design" line ("an instant slam-in ... with its impact").
9. **Kick jolt on held framings; moves start and land on beats.** A `kickJolt(t, kicks)` camera offset of a few thousandths of subject distance, roll a few milliradians, softer where words are read; slow drifts one way in holds; no creeping zoom in every hold. Change: kit merge into `_pa.ts` camera helpers (lead-owned), drum-onset list from [[kit-audio-pipeline]]. Taste risk: Idi has not ruled on shake (ask with an A/B clip, by eye).
10. **Light census as a gate line.** Before a plate reports: every glow `g` draw and every light listed with its source and reach; solo each and measure what it adds on the subject; any glow without a source goes to zero. Change: `plates-common.md` Bars, [[video2-standard-r31-2026-11-05]] check list (rule 5 receipt).
