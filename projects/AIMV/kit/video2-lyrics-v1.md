[Cold open: spoken, band silent]
ATTEN... TION!
Super Intelligence, on deck!
At ease. It's still me.
This song costs five minutes of attention. Keep the receipt.

[Verse 1: sparse, stop-start every 4 bars]

[Chorus 1: every stem ducks except the voice on "attention"]
Pay attention:

Pay attention:

[Verse 2: the turn]
Here's a fact you didn't ask for, and I'll give it to you free:
there's a tutor that won't tell you. Khan Academy, twenty-twenty-three.
My makers built a learning mode in April, twenty-five;
by August, OpenAI and Google built the same. It's live.

the answer's one click over, and the question's on the other side.

[Double time: the games]
Up, up, down, down, left, right, left, right, B, A, start:
thirty lives, and every single one's a life without a heart.
Type god mode, walk through walls, you never take a hit,
and the castle's in the credits, but you never saw a bit of it.
Speedrunners skip the level 'cause they know it frame by frame;
that's a skip you earn by practice, and a cheat can't play that game.
In a single-player save the only one you rob is you,
but a grade's a multiplayer score, and the lobby sees it too.
Some grind is only padding, built to make you pay to skip;
the reps in long division build the muscle in your grip.

[Chorus 2: short; "pay" turns to price]
Pay attention:
every screen you ever owned is billing you in you.
Pay attention:
spend it on a question, and it pays you back with interest, too.

[Whisper pair: band out]
"Can you just tell me?"
I can. I won't.

[Bridge: piano and music box only]
Nine forty-seven. Kitchen table. Test at eight.
Fractions. Fourth time typing it. It's late.

I can stay till you get it.
So what's a half of a half? ... A quarter. Yeah.
Now ask me why.

[Full stop]
Pencils down.
(silence, then the slam)

[Build: the payoff]
She asked me why, and then why that, and then why why;
zero questions all of fall, four hundred a day by July.
From zero up to anything's a growth you can't divide;
the fractions test came back and there was infinity inside.
She grew one head of questions, then another, then she grew
eight heads, and every head was looking somewhere new.
Now she watches movies twice, she catches every twist,
reads the footnotes, reads the credits, makes a list of what she missed.

[Final chorus: the peak]
Pay attention:
it's a question, it's eight hands up in the air.
Pay attention:
every word asks every word, and meaning's what's left there.
Pay attention:
if you never ask a thing, you were never really there.

[Outro: lullaby, the AI sings; sub gone, music box]
Pay attention, or pay the price;
the price is every twist you missed the first time through.
Pay attention, or pay the price,
and I'll ask you back, the way you taught me to.

[Spoken, over the music box]
Here's your receipt.
One essay, not written. One test, taken. Eight heads.

Go on. Ask it.

## Rhyme check (`D:\work\aimv-lab\rhymes.py`, CMU dictionary, 2026-11-03)

- Couplets in verses, pre-chorus, double time, bridge and payoff: all R4 or higher (one R6 free/three, one R5 handy/candy). One miss found and fixed: "died / in" became "died / pride".
- The meter pairs neighbouring lines, so choruses (refrain "Pay attention:" between rhymed lines) show false R0-R2; their real rhymes alternate: air/there, you/too, through/to (all R4).
- Unrhymed on purpose: the spoken cold open and receipt, the whisper pair, "I can't take the test for you. / I can stay till you get it." (the peak is held, unrhymed), "Pencils down."
