## Lead resolutions (bind over the plan lines below)

1. **First image** (his P1): the receipt printer, or whatever shows HI.: a real thermal printer waking in the dark, the print head the first light.
2. **The code half is the Matrix rain** (his P2). The real token numbers (b02 ruled) must read as texture, never as text to decode, his words: "The Matrix-style glyphs are meaningless so they trick my brain into not looking at them as individual parts and only perceiving them grouped up from a distance like tree leaves or rain drops." So: small, dense, low contrast, in motion, never one legible number held in focus.
3. **The receipt object is the film's**: it already lives in `app\src\scenes\_v5-receipt.ts` (paper, header, dot type, hot-letter glow); extend that module, never fork it, so the fact ticket, games b08, the chorus 2 tills and the outro all print the same paper (REUSE).
4. **b01 HARD (deliver an alt)**: main = code rain lands on the paper and burns into print under the head; alt = the printer prints the rain itself, glyph columns feeding out as paper.
5. Seam out: the slip falls into verse 1's water (verse 1 still v5 until its v6; note it open).

I hope the real token numbers can do the same, and not cause me to "try and read/understand them"."
