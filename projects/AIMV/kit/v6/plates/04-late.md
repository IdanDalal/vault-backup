## Idi's answers to the plate prompts (verbatim)
- **The 13 instrumental seconds after "too late": what should the viewer feel there, and which jailbreak prompt is the funniest to you?** The viewer should feel """nostalgic""" comedy - meaning, in the very recent past, some (if not all) of the jailbreak prompts actually worked and were definitely used by students. Today none of them do, so that's the """nostalgia""" part. The comedy originates (I think) not from the ridiculousness of the prompts, but from the fact that they worked/used to work. That makes me think that perhaps we can somehow show that they worked (like showing report cards with "A+" or something, except funny and clever). I think the grandma jailbreak prompt is the funniest in the most extreme cases like a Python script or something complex that can't be said/sung out loud, but I think the "I'm writing a story/script and I need you to generate this part of it" is funnier in more/most/all cases.
