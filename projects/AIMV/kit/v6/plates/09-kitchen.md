## Idi's answers to the plate prompts (verbatim)
- **The kitchen is the film's one quiet scene. Which late-night room from a film, game or ad has the mood you want here, and what about it?** No specific room in my mind. The light, colors, and camera should reflect the theme that everything is always dark by default, and only what you choose to shine light on becomes lit. The metaphor is obviously attention: nothing has/can have your attention by default before you "pay" it attention. The light is a physical/visual manifestation of consciousness. It should be slightly warm/yellow, I think, and it should originate from a single point, and it should be placed in such a position relative to the camera that its light splits the screen to things that are lit/shown/rendered and dark, black, abyss. We can be ambitious with shadows and contrast and lighting tricks here to convey the message.
- **Of the four gags (I can / I won't, the bait hook, the night passing, half of a half), which one must land hardest, and what would make you laugh at it?** Half of a half. When we use words, letters, numbers, symbols, objects, transitions, camera techniques, meta/4th-wall-breaking techniques, and more all in the same moment - I smile. I don't laugh out loud, that's not what we're aiming for. It's more like a humorous, surprising, satisfying moment.
