## 5. Limits, stated

- The effects and restagings stand on a bare stage with no plate around them. They show the idea and the level; how each sits in its plate is phase 2 and 3 work.
- The organic clips' numbers read far lower than any film's because a bare stage has few edges. They compare a level with the other level and with v3 only.
- Colour is painted over frames. The engine renders it for real on the proof plate.
- Playback, sound and tapping in Idi's own browser are untested from here.
- Not run on the demo clips: the plate gates for clarity, object lifecycle, beat sync and flashes. They run on real plates.
- The usage limit stopped both builders at 16:07; they resumed from their own transcripts at 18:02 with nothing lost. For the plan: phase 0 plus 57 minutes of phase 1 with two builders used up the window.
