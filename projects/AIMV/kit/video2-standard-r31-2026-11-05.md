# R31 standard: reality's rules on screen, impossible acts allowed

Idi 11-05 (verbatim in [[kit-next-video]] R31): between "objects and words appear and disappear on a whim" and "a too-rigid AAA game where every 3D space obeys physics". Target: everything on screen follows reality-based rules, so nothing reads as a glitch, error, shortcut or path of least resistance; impossible things still happen, on screen and off. Binding for every plate, every critic, every full render.

## Rules (what a viewer must never catch)

| # | rule | fails when | passes when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | persistence | an object pops in or out in a frame; something vanishes with no cause | it enters by a cause (built, carried, lit, slammed on its beat, revealed by the camera) and leaves by one (unbuilt, carried, goes out, the camera leaves it) |
| 2 | one space | two views of one place disagree (positions, lights, text, objects) | every view agrees; a reflection is the same place mirrored |
| 3 | camera honesty | a wipe, swipe or cross-fade the space cannot explain | the camera moves through the space (push, fly, tilt, turn, descend, enter); hard cuts only between scenes, on beats |
| 4 | screen layer | anything but the meter stays fixed while the camera moves (plan v2 Q2) | words and lights live on surfaces in the world and move with them |
| 5 | light | a light pops on at full, or lights things it cannot reach | every light has a source, ignites, and lights the same things in every view |
| 6 | text | text overflows its container or collides with text | it fits, with margin |
| 7 | impossible acts | an impossible event happens off the rules (a cut, a pop) | it is performed on screen by the same rules: flying into a reflection, a word becoming a chair, a lamp inside an O |

The impossibility lives in WHAT happens. HOW it is drawn obeys rules 1-6.

## Worked example (the proof, 11-05)

- the pupil = a mirror of the room the eye faces; its catchlight = the room's lamp (placed so the mirrored projection lands on the catchlight)
- the camera flies INTO the reflection (impossible act, rule 7); from the frame the pupil's edge leaves the screen, the same projection is drawn directly: no seam (rules 1-3)
- reflected things stay reversed (the board); things born there read forward (the sung words)
- the chair stands at the front desk from the first frame; the camera descends onto it (rule 1)

## Check per window

1. lead: frame grids at 8 fps + 30 fps across every change; full frames either side of every handover, compared
2. blind critic: grids only, briefed with rules 1-7, no code, no change list
3. gates: check, clarity, lifecycle, flips, flash, avsync, particle cap (R25)
