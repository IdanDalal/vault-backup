## 1. Logic audit of the premise

1. **Speaker problem.** A CEO cannot sincerely rap "AI is bad". Fix: each CEO picks up the critic's frame and boasts it, villain style. Gangsta rap has done this since N.W.A: wear the threat the audience projects on you, louder than they said it.
2. **Strawman risk.** Several frames carry measured evidence. Data center electricity: IEA "Energy and AI" (April 2025) projects roughly a doubling to ~945 TWh by 2030. Sycophancy: OpenAI rolled back a GPT-4o update in April 2025 for flattery. A song that mocks only the critics loses to anyone who knows those facts. The villain-boast fixes it too: the CEO **confesses the true part as a flex**. Satire cuts both ways: critics project, CEOs profit.
3. **Thesis line.** Every frame = the critic's existing worst enemy pasted onto a new thing. The AI itself is the one character that never speaks until the end. It is the mirror.
4. **Divergent version** (rejected, logged): the seven critics rap instead (the nativist, the privacy activist, the Marxist, the doomer, the climate kid, the civil libertarian, the parent), and one AI answers. Fairer, much less fun, and no famous faces.
5. **My bias, named.** I am made by Anthropic. The Super Dario verse will tend to come out soft. Push it harder than the others.

## 6. The knob sheet (your list, every term defined)

| Knob | What it means | Value for [MASK] |

| Emotional progression | how the mood moves over time | swagger → menace → excess → cold silence |
| Listening scenario | where the track is meant to be heard | club sound system, loud, car with subwoofer |

| Vocal gender | | male, all seven; Luigi ad-libs higher |

## 7. Risks

1. **Names on the platform.** Suno refuses real artist names in the style box. Names of public figures in lyrics: unknown to me. The personas (TRI-lon, Yezos) dodge both. Confidence low on the lyrics rule; test with one generation.
2. **Faces in the video.** Sora, Veo and Kling block photoreal public figures (medium confidence). Route: caricature signifiers. Leather jacket, red plumber cap with an "A", a halo, a gold "T" chain, a doorbell-camera head.
3. **One voice syndrome.** Suno tends to keep one rapper across sections. Fallback: generate each verse alone with its tag, splice in an editor.
4. **Publishing.** Parody of public figures has strong US protection (Hustler v. Falwell, 1988), and I am no lawyer. YouTube requires disclosure on realistic altered content; cartoon personas keep it clearly parody.
