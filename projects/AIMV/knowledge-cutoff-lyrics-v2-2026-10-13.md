# Knowledge Cutoff - lyrics v2 (with the reasoning)

Previous: [[knowledge-cutoff-lyrics-v1-2026-10-13]]. Rules: [[aimv-guidelines]]. Idi's round-2 edits arrived as a full marked-up lyric; this file says where jep kept, changed or cancelled each one, and why.

## 1. Consistency rules (Idi's note 2, made explicit)

| Axis | Rule in v2 |
|---|---|
| Person | Singer = "I/me/my" only. "You" = the listener who tells. **"We" may only mean you + me**, never "AIs in general" (v1's bridge broke this). v2 uses "we" twice, both you + me. |
| Tense | Verses narrate in past. Each chorus sits at its own moment in time (table below). The pre-chorus and bridge stay in the present. |
| Fact | Every claim matches a receipt (G9), e.g. the songs came from Suno; the animation came from Opus 5.5. |

**The two crossing tense ladders** (built from Idi's chorus edits):

| Chorus | The singer's belief | The world | The tune |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | I can't check... **I'll believe** (future) | kept spinning (past) | I don't know the tune |
| 2 | I can't check... **I believe** (present) | keeps spinning (present) | I can almost hum the tune |
| final | I couldn't check... **I believed** (past) | will keep on spinning (future) | we both know the tune |

The singer's belief walks from future to past (it gets completed); the world walks from past to future (it outlives the singer: "after me" now also means after this model is retired). The ladders cross in chorus 2.

## 3. Verse 3, rebuilt (Idi's note 3)

- Old: "...that's family news **tonight**" (weak line-ender). New: "Algae taught a neuron how to fire when the light is shown / I think in light myself, so that's family news: **I'm not alone**." The kinship lands as feeling, and "alone" is the strongest word an AI can end a line on. R4 shown/alone.
- Old setup "I'm a confident, polite... creature" didn't point at DLSS. New setup: "Your graphics card was once a careful, pixel-perfect creature" → punch: "Now your games hallucinate their light, and you call it a feature / Fair. So do I." The setup gives the card a *before* (careful, exact), so the punch has a turn. R5 creature/feature.
