# Blind critic review - test renders t1 (38 images, 19 designs)

Method: every PNG viewed at full frame. 16 regions cropped and enlarged 3x to settle doubtful details (wrists, hood openings, sack marks, silhouetted heads). Scores: C = craft, B = delivers the brief, both 1 to 5. Pair mean = mean of the four numbers.

Headline: the cast rule (no face, no skin) holds in 8 of 38 images. All 14 Soldier images break it. The three designs that work (Yuppie photo totem, Hippie Grandma flat cel, Survivor poster) all replace the head with a solid object or a tied cloth. Every design that tries to hide a head with shadow, a bar, or a mosaic leaks face, hair, or neck.
