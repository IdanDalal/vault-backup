# Session 3 · Mon 08-17 · K (9), D (12)

**This page is the storyteller. You only play the character. When lost, glance here.**

## Before 9:00
- [ ] Tape the 6-phrase card by the board: **Your turn · Write it · Check · One more · Well done · Finished**
- [ ] Two marker colors out · TTS app ready · board clean
- [ ] Read the two experiment boxes once, aloud

## Director's booth (storyteller duty, 10 seconds each time)
Booth = activity boundaries only. Inside an activity: play the character, trust the plan.
At every boundary, glance here and run 3 checks:
1. Is she producing English (saying or writing anything)?
2. What's next? Read it off this page.
3. What does she get to choose? Pick from Sprinkles.

**Loop alarm:** explaining the same thing a 3rd time = forced booth visit. Side with her against the tool ("את צודקת, זה מבלבל, טעות שלי"), drop one level of abstraction, or switch activity.

## The catch-me game (30-second reveal, then it runs itself)
Say: "גם אני לומד פה. ה-AI שלי מקשיב להקלטות ונותן לי שיעורי בית. ההרגל שאני מתקן השבוע: אני אומר 'נכון?' כל הזמן. אם תפסתן אותי, תגידו 'תפסתי אותך!', נסמן קו בפינת הלוח, אני אגיד תודה וממשיכים."
- **One habit only this session: נכון?** (קל joins the game next week)
- Ritual capped at 5 seconds: catch → tally mark → "תודה!" → back to the task. Smile every time.
- Her tally corner = your live score. Day target: under 20.

## Experiment #2 · WAIT
> [!tip] Ask → thumb presses 3 fingertips under the table → only then speak.
> - Name it once: "אני שואל ואז מחכה קצת. זה בכוונה."
> - 3 seconds passed? Shrink the question: offer 2 options to choose between. That replaces answering it yourself.
> - Wait after her answer too. She will add more.

## Experiment #4 · FIX CHAIN
> [!tip] Her hand makes every correction.
> 1. Point: "משהו פה לא מדויק. מה?"
> 2. Narrow: "תסתכלי על האות ה___"
> 3. Supply last, and she writes or says the fixed form back.

## D (12) · first ~30 min
1. **Frontier probe**, 3 min, oral, no stakes: *again · wait · wrong · win · lose · early · late · together*. Misses become today's set.
2. Script the misses into one **"watching a stream"** scene; act it out.
3. Sentence game: coherent word picks only, or declare **"spot the nonsense"** mode out loud.
4. **Squishy time = English time.** Board frames: `This is ___. It is ___ and ___.` She fills. You wait.

## K (9) · second ~35 min
1. **Reprobe** Wednesday's finds: *olive · oil · flour · well done* (2 hits = hers for good).
2. **Snack script**, physical, in the kitchen: *bread · cheese · cut · plate · hot · eat*.
3. **Hunt round 2**: she picks the shelf or drawer.
4. **Dictation loop** (TTS → she writes → fix chain). Densest segment; protect it. This is the נכון-free zone.
5. **Closing ceremony:** you photograph = saving the game. **She erases** = her official job.

## Sprinkles (trivial choices, hand out freely)
which marker color means NEW · which word first · which shelf · which squishy stars in the sentence · where on the board · who presses TTS

## Word swaps
- "קל" → deleted. Only the girls may say it.
- Hard word coming → "המילה הבאה טריקית" (failure painless, success a win)
- "נכון?" → a question whose answer is an English word
- Praise → quiet + process: "שמת לב ל___"
- Your stories → 20 seconds, then end on a question handed back to her

## Ending
Thanks + "אתן עוזרות לי ללמוד איך ללמד" · clean goodbye · the recording grades everything else (talk share target: under 60%)
