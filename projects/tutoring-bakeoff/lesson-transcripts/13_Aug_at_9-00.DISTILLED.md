## jep analysis (2026-08-14, discussed with Idan)

1. **Loanword saturation**: word-sourcing from slang/room harvest structurally guarantees already-known words (English loanwords in Hebrew = ambient vocabulary). The kitchen hunt found new words because packaging text is functional, not borrowed. Frontier = non-loanword English adjacent to known loanwords → app needs a frontier-finder pre-test, not a "which do you know" checklist.
2. **Dictation loop is the template**: production + immediate feedback + physical writing. Scale it.
3. **Choice at boundaries, structure inside**: the autonomy moves worked at activity boundaries; the best segment was tightly structured. Choose-then-commit.
4. **Graph split**: planning projection stays with Idan; kids get a concrete past-facing counter (words-learned jar/list).
5. **Escalation encoded**: the 32:00 live pivot (too easy → hunt) is the escalation mechanism; encode as per-activity fallback cards ("knows 80%+ → skip ahead"). Repair move for tool confusion: side with the kid against the artifact ("you're right, this chart is confusing"), never re-explain a third time.
6. **Medium is Hebrew**: 73% tutor talk, in Hebrew; production is the gap. Convert passion topics (squishies) into small English production asks.
7. **Sentence generator**: constrain to coherent word groups, or make "spot the nonsense" an explicit game mode.
8. **Ending + transparency**: goodbye ritual landed; AI transparency and report-philosophy transparency both landed with D.
