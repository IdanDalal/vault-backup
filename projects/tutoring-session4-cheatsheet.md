## The reveal (30 sec, once per girl — this is the hook, spend it)
> "בניתי לך משחק. הוא נבנה רק בשבילך, עם המילים שלך. והכי חשוב: את יכולה לשנות בו הכל. את הופכת להיות המתכנתת."
Then hand over the laptop and shut up. She presses, she discovers. No tour.

## How the game teaches (your eyes only — never explain this to them)
- **Gate** (crash / start): picture appears → she must TYPE the English word to fly again. Meaning-first retrieval.
- **Skeleton ladder**: blanks → first letter after miss 1 → full word after miss 2. The escalation ladder runs itself; you stay quiet.
- **Tokens**: picture+word pairs fly by mid-game = reading exposure that primes the next gate.
- **Storm** (mid-level): two words back-to-back under pressure; success = heart back + double rainbow.
- **Bonus** (sunrise): every caught word shown 2 sec, then typed from memory = delayed retrieval.
- **Password**: finishing sets a word for NEXT session's door. Spaced retrieval across days, built in.

## The gate protocol (the whole pedagogy lives here)
1. Picture appears → **count 3 full seconds**. Silence. Give her time to read the picture on her own.
2. House rule, say it once at the start: **"המשחק שומע רק אם אומרים את המילה בקול לפני שמקלידים."** (say-then-type = production before spelling)
3. She misses twice and copies the revealed word → that's the game being kind. No comment, no "easy". Next gate is a fresh try.
4. She's stuck AND frustrated → escape 2: blame the game ("הציור הזה מבלבל, אשמתי"), then swap that word in the config together. The swap doubles as her first taste of the remake.

# D (12) · first ~30 min

### D1. 60-second frontier check (oral, before the laptop opens)
Her game words: **AGAIN · WRONG · WAIT · TOGETHER · BOUNCY · SOFT**. Say each in English, she gives the meaning.
- Word she owns cold twice in a row → it's harvest. Swap it during the remake (D3) for one she missed.
- Backups at her frontier: **almost · both · other · ready · still · enough**

### D2. First runs (~10 min, she plays, you vanish)
- Her file: SQUISHY SKY, candy world, teddy player, party music. Storm hits mid-level; bonus at sunrise.
- Your only lines are English game furniture, spoken as the game's voice: **"Wait… wait… GO!" · "Wrong! Try again!" · "Together!"** — her genre words, out loud, in context.
- She wins → the game prints her password for next time. Read it together, once: "זו המילה שתפתח את המשחק בפעם הבאה."

### D3. The remake (~10 min — this is the real lesson)
Open the file's CHANGE ME block in a text editor, side by side. English words are the control panel.
- She changes any 3: `player` emoji · `world` · `speed` · `music` · `trail` color · her `name:` line. Every knob is a sprinkle.
- **The word swap**: replace one harvest word in `words:` with a frontier word from D1. Then she picks its emoji for the PICTURE BOOK — her picture, her word. ("איזה ציור מתאים ל-ALMOST?") That negotiation IS the vocabulary lesson.
- Save, refresh, her change is live. "את שינית את המשחק" — quiet, factual.

### D4. Board echo + counter (3 min, closes her block)
Laptop closed. "אילו מילים תפסת היום?" She writes them on the whiteboard from memory — say aloud, then write. FIX CHAIN on errors (point → narrow → supply last, her hand only). **She updates her counter column herself.**
