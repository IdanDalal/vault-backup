## Bottom line

- **One-day hit:** pool -$1,272 (-4.48%) on 10-22. 30 days: positions -7.0%, mostly SKHY (-$382) and MU (-$240).
- **Trigger, high confidence:** two reports on Wed 10-21 after the close, read together as an "AI financing scare":
  - **Meta:** first negative free cash flow since 2012 (-$2.12B), 2026 capex floor raised to $135B, no 2027 number, new non-voting share class.
  - **Lam Research:** supply limits (clean rooms, components) cap output into 2027.
  - Add Jensen Huang on 10-22: neoclouds lack working capital.
- **Size:** chip index SOX -6.72%, all 30 members down, worst day since June 23. Nasdaq-100 -2.9%, S&P 500 -1.2%. The equal-weight S&P rose +0.73%.
- **Overlay, medium confidence:** Brent +4% to $113 on Iran / Hormuz / Houthi news. Money moved into staples (+3.3%), utilities (+2.1%), health care (+1.3%).
- **Rates were not the 10-22 trigger:** 10-year yield fell about 17 bp to about 4.97%.
- **The one-month slide is older:** hot September CPI (3.7%, out 10-14), a September Fed hike, the 10-year above 5.30% in early October. SOX lost 8 sessions in a row to 10-16 (longest run since 2008).
- **Counter-reading, medium confidence:** chips were +57% YTD by mid-October and crowded; Asia fell first (Kospi -3.5%). This fits positioning-driven selling with the headlines as the spark. No positioning data checked.
