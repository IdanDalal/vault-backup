# No Boilerplate - Obsidian for Learning III: Projects

jep laid out the highlights. The space under each one is Idi's, and jep leaves it alone. Tick `processed` when the source is spent.

## Highlights

### 1. [0:40] The one question

> "The single thing I need my task systems to do, from a simple shopping list to complex project management software, is to answer the question, 'What should I be doing right now?'"



### 2. [1:45] Win the day

> "What single task would be the thing that if you finished would kind of win the day?"



### 3. [3:05] A project is ordered by time

> "Within the project, the list is no longer ordered by importance. The list is by time, sequentially."



### 4. [5:10] Next action

> "A project's next action is the first unchecked checkbox in the file, the first to do, the first thing that is not yet done, the thing you've got to do next."



### 5. [6:50] Scope the day

> "It's vital to not just capture and plan exhaustively as we have done in the first part of this video, but then to cut down and tightly scope the day's tasks to only the things we really care about, only the things that can be started."



### 6. [9:00] Contexts are mental

> "The solution to the context problem is to make the delineation mental, not physical."



### 7. [12:45] The warning

> "You're building this system to actually get work done, not to organize yourself endlessly. Beware of the seductive optimizations of toxic productivity."



## jep's notes

- His six layers, simple to complex, "allowing you to drop off when you think the complexity is right for you": daily note, project files with a due date, next actions, start date, state (a Kanban from Bases), contexts. His five contexts come from the "Eudaimonia Machine" in Cal Newport's *Deep Work*: Chamber, Library, Office, Salon, Theater.
- Checked: the Bases Kanban view is real (Obsidian 1.14.0 early access 2026-09-02, public 1.14.4 on 2026-10-05). "First unchecked checkbox" as a column is unavailable in Bases, which reads frontmatter only; the cards carry a `next` property instead.
- Measured: nothing. "90% of productivity" is his estimate.
- In the workshop: layers 2, 3 and 5 are the cards, the Now table and the Board. His contexts became `ball` (whose move: Idi, jep, the 4090).
