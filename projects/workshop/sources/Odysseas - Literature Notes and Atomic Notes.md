# Odysseas - Effective Note-Taking to Remember More + Create New Ideas

Listed in Idi's `PKMS\PKM.txt`. Longer companion: [[Odysseas - Obsidian The King of Learning Tools]].

## What it says

- [1:10] Two note kinds do the whole job.
- **Literature note**: while reading, write down what stood out, in your own words, with the page number. Punchy quotes allowed. For fiction: what a character does, or what you felt.
- [2:00] "You have to write in your own words." Three benefits he names: it forces thinking; it tests understanding ("if you can't explain it in your own words then you don't understand it"); it improves memory because "learning actually is helped by friction."
- [5:50] **Atomic note**: one idea lifted out of a literature note and stripped of its context, so it reads alone. His example: from Aeschylus, *Prometheus Bound* (Zeus is harsh after taking power) comes the note "if you enter power you're harsh to maintain it", with no Zeus in it. That note can then meet Machiavelli's *The Prince*.
- [8:20] New ideas come from atomic notes meeting each other. Software with clickable links makes the meeting cheap.
