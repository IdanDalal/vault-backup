## 3. Home-game and recurring small-group formats (start or join)

- **Home poker, legal status (Israel Penal Law 1977, sections 224-230)** | "prohibited game" = any game where a person can win money, money's worth, or a benefit and the result depends more on chance than skill (s.224); organizing one = up to 3 years (s.225); playing in one = up to 1 year (s.226); exemption (s.230): a game aimed at a specific group of people, within "the bounds of amusement or entertainment", and not held at a regular gambling venue. Courts have treated poker as chance-based: Jerusalem District Court convicted a couple of keeping a gambling venue over a living-room Texas Hold'em game with friends (Time Out interview, 2015). A Knesset bill to license poker tournaments exists; status unconfirmed | https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/073_002.htm (current law text), https://timeout.co.il/שיחה-עם-יור-איגוד-הפוקר-על-מעמדו-החוקי/ (2015-12-31) | verdict (medium confidence, not legal advice): **no-money poker (chips only, no buy-in, prize = bragging rights or the recap) falls outside "prohibited game" because nobody can win money or money's worth**; small-stakes friends' games lean on the s.230 amusement exemption, which is a gray zone given the 2015 conviction. Fit: HIGH as a no-stakes format. His proven asset is the written recap, which needs no money on the table.
- **Recurring group formats he could start** (poker without stakes, co-op video game night, film club with a written recap, Letterboxd/critique club) | what guides converge on: start small (5-10 people), fix one weekday that avoids clashes, one named host/greeter, agree the scope up front ("be clear what members are signing up for" cuts dropouts), check whether a local group already exists before founding one | https://www.intofilm.org/news-and-views/articles/9-tips-on-starting-a-film-club, https://hunterfiber.com/start-a-movie-club/, https://zatu.com/en-au/blogs/features/board-game-club-part-3 | informal practitioner guides (no controlled evidence) | fit: HIGH on paper; his poker era (94 recaps, 4 years) already ran this exact template. Risk: his home is not a hosting venue (TELOS), so the host seat needs a free space (library room, community center "matnas", a cafe that welcomes games) [UNVERIFIED which ones].
- **Time budget evidence for any recurring group** | Jeffrey Hall (2018, Journal of Social and Personal Relationships, 355 adults who had moved + college freshmen): ~40-60 h together to reach casual friend, ~80-100 h to friend, 200+ h to close friend; leisure time (joking, playing video games) counts, work-alongside time counts far less | https://news.ku.edu/2018/03/06/study-reveals-number-hours-it-takes-make-friend | 2018, single survey study, self-report, not a replication | implication: a weekly 3-hour group reaches casual-friend hours in ~4-5 months; a once-a-year festival shift never gets there alone.
