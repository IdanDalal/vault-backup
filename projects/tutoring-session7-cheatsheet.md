# Monday cheatsheet: session 7, K (9) and D (12), 2026-09-07 14:00 to 15:00, two stations, menu of generators

Read once with coffee. Verdicts and install steps: [[tutoring-session7-verdicts]], [[tutoring-session7-quest]]. Records this is built on: S6 (08-31) generative segment, S5 (08-24), P6 counter rule.

## The 60-second version

1. Two stations, fixed at 14:03 by rock-paper-scissors, swap at 14:28 on the phone timer. Inside her station she picks any generator, any time, in any order.
2. Your hands never touch a keyboard or a phone screen from 14:00 to 15:00. You spell aloud, you write on the whiteboard, she types. S6 score was Idan 7 typed / girls 5. Monday target: Idan 0.
3. Every accepted word she typed goes on her own word card, in her hand, at 14:50. Monotonic. No sister comparison.
4. Nothing from photos of the girls. Drawings, objects, and typed words only.
5. When a card below says "say the line", say the line. The lines are the structure; improvising is where S5 and S6 leaked.

## Before 14:00 (10 min, done alone)

- PC: games closed (the GPU needs ~20 GB free), ComfyUI running, then double-click `D:\work\studio\kidstudio\start.bat` (page opens full screen by itself), ComfyUI's own tab closed, PC audio on, `reset.bat` run if the word lists are not at zero. Warm up all three yourself before they arrive: one picture (`red cat`), one SPEAK line, one 30 s song; the first of each is slower. PIN in `pin.txt`.

Write those numbers at the top of each card. Whiteboard, one marker. Kitchen timer or phone timer.
- Whiteboard, top line, in English: `TYPE · SPELL? · NEW WORD · SEND`. Bottom line: the menu (below).
- Two chairs at the PC keyboard for the girls, zero chairs for you. You stand or sit on the sofa with the marker.

## Prompt cards (words at their level; every card has a one-word floor so nobody stalls)

**PICTURE (PC, Kid Studio).** Floor: one word. Ladder: `cat` → `pink cat` → `pink cat dancing` → `pink cat dancing in school`. Style buttons on screen are English and she reads them: CARTOON · ANIME · CLAY · PIXEL · 3D · WATERCOLOR · CRAYON · STICKER · COMIC · LEGO · NEON · PLUSH, plus SHAPE and HOW MANY. The SEED: SAME button is a teaching move: same picture, change one word, see what the word does. Words that worked in S5/S6: cat, dragon, school, fish, squishy, flower, sun, water, gold coins, piggy bank, ice cream, horse, pink car, pet. A red word on screen means "not on my list yet": she reads it, you write it on the board, she types it again once you press the plus. Every red word is a spelling episode on purpose.

**SPEAK (PC, same page, SPEAK tab).** Floor: `hello`. Ladder: `My name is K` → `My name is K. I love squishies.` → three lines. Voice buttons are names she reads (HEART, BELLA, SKY, ADAM, SANTA, EMMA, LILY, GEORGE and more) plus a SPEED slider; the player has SPEED x0.5 for hearing every sound. She types, the PC says it back in an accent she picked. The one place where a sentence, with a capital and a full stop, is the payoff.

**SONG (PC; her phone app as the alternative).** Floor: two lines, Enter between them. Buttons: 12 genres (POP, ROCK, KPOP, CHIPTUNE, LULLABY...), SINGER (GIRL, BOY, KIDS, ROBOT), MOOD, TEMPO, LENGTH slider 10 to 90 s (30 s is ~9 s of waiting). The page adds the `[verse]` tag itself. The GALLERY tab replays anything she made today on the TV. Template on the board: `I love ___ / I go to ___ / I want ___`. S6 hits: "My name is D, I'm 10 years old, I'm excited to go to school tomorrow", "I love squishies", "I love to swim". Song about a sister only if that sister asks for it.

**DRAW→VIDEO (phone, Meta AI).** She photographs her own drawing (closer, more light: the S6 dragon retake), types `bring my drawing to life` then one verb: `fly`, `dance`, `swim`, `run`, `jump`. Refusal script is card F3. Wait is 1 to 3 minutes: the rule is "send it, then go do IMAGE on your phone while it cooks", never staring at the spinner.

**LISTEN (PC, dictation).** She presses the ear, hears a sentence (often one she wrote), types it, GO paints each letter green or red. Floor: two-word sentences come up on their own. Your line when a letter is red: "איזו אות? תקשיבי שוב" and she presses the ear again. Never type it for her; the ear replays as many times as she wants.

**Opener on the PC.** When she taps her color, three of her own words appear on the stage. "תקישי על מילה, תשמעי אותה, תקלידי אחת". That is the warm-up; no whiteboard probe needed.

**STORY (PC, STORY tab, added 09-07 11:00).** The one thing to finish: 4 frames, then a movie. The page asks the question per frame (BEGINNING who is it? where are they? · PROBLEM what goes wrong? · TRY what do they do about it? · END how does it end?); she fills the picture slots plus one SAY IT line, GO makes the picture and speaks her line (about 5 s), the strip fills up. The hero is locked from frame 2 (SAME HERO button unlocks). After 2 or more frames, MAKE THE MOVIE stitches pictures, her voice, and her newest song of the day into one mp4 in about 2 s; it plays on the stage and KEEP sends it to the phone. Floor: one word in WHO OR WHAT and one word in SAY IT. If she stalls on a slot: tap a YOU CAN signpost, the page speaks the question, you say nothing. Line to say when frame 4 is done: "עכשיו הסרט. תלחצי". Song first (SONG tab, 20 s) if she wants music under it.

**Slots everywhere (09-07).** PICTURE, SPEAK, SONG and STORY all show 4 boxes instead of one: what she MUST decide. The dashed YOU CAN chips under the active box are what she CAN decide (ANGLE, HOW MANY, RHYME...): they ask a question aloud and never type for her. Your role is unchanged: spell aloud, she types.

**TEXT→VIDEO.** Off the menu Monday (local: minutes per clip; phone: unproven). Quest A7 Sunday result on Idan's phone: ______ (if Meta AI made a video from text, write "phone, 1 to 3 min" here and it joins station 2). If asked otherwise: "בשבוע הבא, זה עוד לא מוכן".

## The counter ritual

- During: every accepted word she typed appears in her list on the Kid Studio page (K or D profile) and on her phone chat. You never write on her card.
- 14:50, both stations stop, timer rings. Each girl reads her list aloud, one word at a time, and copies the new ones to her own card. New total in her hand, under the old one. Line to say: "עכשיו את יכולה לכתוב 23, לפני שבועיים 17". Nothing about the sister's number, ever.
- Words you spelled count. Words you typed do not exist (you typed nothing).
- Phone-station words: she reads them off her WhatsApp chat history; same rule.

## Fallback cards (read the card, do the card)

**F1 K demands an audience ("עידן, תבוא!").** Say: "שתי דקות, אני מתחיל טיימר". Go, look, say one English word about what you see, ask one question in English ("what is it? what color?"), leave at the beep. Next time she calls inside 5 minutes: "עוד 3 דקות, עדיין בתור של D". The timer is the boss, so you are not.

**F2 D asks you to type ("אתה יכול לרשום לי?").** Say: "אני מאיית, את מקלידה". Write the word on the whiteboard in capitals, hold it up, she copies. If she pushes: "המחשב מחכה לך, לא לי". Wait. Silence is fine; the generator does nothing until she types, and that is the design. Never take the keyboard, never lean over her hands.

**F3 A generator refuses or stalls.** Refusal (Meta AI said no, or a red word on the PC): "האפליקציה אמרה לא. איזו מילה? תחליפי מילה אחת". One swap, she picks it, you spell. Stall past 60 s on the PC: press her station's other button (SPEAK) and come back. Stall past 3 min on Meta AI: "שלחנו, זה מתבשל, בינתיים IMAGE". PC dead entirely: station 1 becomes the laptop with Game Lab and the phone menu moves to two phones; the session continues, the record notes the outage.

**F4 Both want the same station.** Say: "הטיימר מחליף ב-14:28. מי שמחכה בוחרת ראשונה בסיבוב הבא". If it escalates: both girls at station 2 for one round with one phone each, station 1 empty for 5 minutes, then re-pick. An empty station costs less than a fight for it.

**F5 (extra) A girl wants a photo of herself or her sister generated.** "לא עושים תמונות של ילדים, גם לא אני. ציור שלך כן". Card F3 follows.

## Five things to watch for (write them in the record after)
